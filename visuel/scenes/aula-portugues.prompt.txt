A real photograph of a classroom of a Portuguese language school for foreigners in Lisbon on a November evening, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a student would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at sitting eye height from the back row, looking towards the whiteboard and the teacher, everything in sharp focus. White ceiling lights and the dark blue evening in the windows; the textures of real laminate desks, plastic chairs, a whiteboard, paper, pens, cotton, wool, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the whiteboard is plain white with only two marker drawings on it, the books are plain single colours with blank covers, the posters are plain coloured rectangles, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- two desks in the first row of the picture; on the left one, an open orange notebook, a pen and a dictionary;
- the young man of the reference pictures sits at the left desk, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he leans towards his neighbour and argues with one finger raised, frowning;
- at the right desk, his classmate, a young man of twenty-seven in a green cap and a yellow t-shirt, argues back with both hands open, frowning too.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- two more students watch them with amusement: an older woman with grey braids and a young man with round glasses;
- a coat rack by the window with three coats and an umbrella.

THE FRONT OF THE CLASSROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- the whiteboard, with two identical simple drawings of a city bus in blue marker, one on the left and one on the right, and a large blue equals sign between them;
- the teacher, a woman of fifty with short grey hair and a bright yellow scarf, stands between the two drawings, arms crossed, eyebrows raised, waiting for them to finish;
- a clock above the board and a map of Portugal and Brazil side by side on the wall, plain green shapes on plain blue.

The classroom is small and bright: between the desks, the same bare floor and the same bare white walls.
