A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of a quiet room of an old art museum in Berlin on a rainy Sunday afternoon, taken as a visitor standing in the doorway a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft grey daylight from a tall window on the right and warm light from the lamps above the paintings. The textures of real oil paint, gilded wood, marble, oak parquet, velvet, plaster, paper, fabric and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a dark-red knitted sweater, a grey wool skirt, black tights and black ankle boots.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the paintings have no labels and no plates beside them, the pages of the sketchbook show only a pencil drawing, and nothing in the room carries a sign, a logo or a brand.

THE MIDDLE of the picture: a long low wooden bench with a dark-green velvet seat in the middle of the room.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures sits on the bench, seen whole from her head to her ankle boots, which rest on the parquet with their shadow under them. She holds an open sketchbook on her knees with one hand and draws with a pencil in the other hand, looking up at the marble sculpture in front of her with a focused smile. Her black backpack stands on the floor beside the bench, and her grey wool coat lies folded on the bench next to her.

THE FRONT of the picture, on the left: a white marble sculpture of a sitting dog, on a square white stone pedestal.

THE LEFT WALL, painted dark green, with three paintings in heavy gold frames, each with a small brass lamp above it:
- a large landscape with snowy mountains and a blue lake;
- a portrait of an old woman in a black dress and a white collar;
- a still life of red apples and yellow flowers on a table.

THE BACK WALL, also dark green:
- a very large painting of a sailing ship on a stormy sea, in a gold frame;
- a high doorway leading to the next room, where another painting can be seen;
- an old museum guard, a man of about sixty-five with white hair and a moustache, in a dark-blue uniform jacket, sits on a wooden chair beside the doorway with his hands on his knees.

THE RIGHT SIDE of the picture:
- a tall window with white frames and the grey rain and a few bare trees outside, with heavy dark-red curtains pulled to the sides;
- a young man of about twenty-five with a short beard, in a brown corduroy jacket and jeans, with white headphones over his ears and a backpack on his back, stands looking at a painting of a horse in a gold frame on the right wall;
- a tall plant in a stone pot in the corner.

THE CEILING is high and white with a moulded border, and a large old chandelier with small white lights hangs in the middle of the room. THE FLOOR is old oak parquet in a herringbone pattern.
