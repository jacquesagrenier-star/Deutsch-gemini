A photorealistic photograph of a fruit stall at an outdoor weekly market in Berlin on a bright, slightly overcast morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens. Taken with a 35 mm lens at a customer's eye height, looking down a little onto the stall, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from behind, the second from the front: her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are her everyday clothes.

THE STALL fills the picture from the left edge to the right edge and from just below the top edge down to the bottom edge. It is a long sloping table under a plain dark-green awning, with the fruit displayed in sixteen open shallow crates of plain raw wood, four crates side by side in each of four rows that climb towards the back, every crate tilted towards the camera so that its fruit is fully visible. Each crate holds ONE kind of fruit, heaped and generous, and the fruit itself is the only thing that tells the crates apart: the crates are bare wood, the awning is plain green cloth, and every fruit shows its own bare skin.
