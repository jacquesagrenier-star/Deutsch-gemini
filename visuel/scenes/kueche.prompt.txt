A real photograph of the kitchen of a shared flat in an old Berlin building on a weekday evening, taken as a flatmate standing in the doorway would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm light from a lamp hanging over the table and the last daylight from a window on the right. The textures of real wood, enamel, steel, ceramic, glass, vegetables, fabric and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a light-grey T-shirt and dark jeans, with a dark-blue cooking apron tied over them.

Every object in the kitchen stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. All the surfaces are plain: the bottles, the jars, the boxes and the appliances carry no label, no print, no display and no writing.

THE BACK WALL, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a tall white fridge with its door closed and a small green plant in a pot on top of it;
- a stretch of light wooden worktop with a white electric stove set into it: four black hotplates, and below them the glass door of the oven;
- on the stove, a large red pot with its lid lying beside it on the worktop, steam rising from the pot, and on another hotplate a black frying pan with two fried eggs in it;
- next to the stove, a white sink with a tap, and beside the sink a white dishwasher under the worktop;
- above the worktop, a wooden shelf with three white plates standing on edge, two glasses, a white cup and a round wall clock above the shelf;
- on the far right, the window with a white frame, a basil plant on the sill, and a blue-and-white checked tea towel hanging from the handle of the oven.

THE TABLE fills the lower half of the picture: a square wooden kitchen table seen from slightly above, with two wooden chairs. On it, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a wooden chopping board with a carrot, half cut into round slices, and an onion cut in half on it;
- three whole potatoes and two red tomatoes;
- a bottle of olive oil with no label and a small dish of butter;
- a white bowl with two green apples and two bananas, their skins plain and unmarked;
- a glass bottle of milk and a loaf of bread on a wooden board;
- a salt shaker and a pepper mill;
- a wooden spoon and a ladle lying side by side;
- an open cookbook with plain blank pages that carry only pictures of vegetables, lying flat at the edge of the table.

A grey cat sits on the seat of the chair on the near left, its paws on the seat, looking at the table. A white towel hangs over the back of the other chair. A small pedal bin stands on the floor next to the fridge.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures stands behind the table, between the table and the stove, turned three-quarters towards the camera; the far edge of the table crosses him at the hips, and above it he is seen whole up to his hair. He is caught in the middle of cooking: he holds a kitchen knife in one hand and slices the carrot on the chopping board, holding it with the other hand, fingertips curled in. He looks up towards the steaming pot with a half-smile that reaches his eyes, as if the soup already smells good.

The walls are painted a pale sage green, the floor is old wooden boards, and nothing on the walls carries any writing.
