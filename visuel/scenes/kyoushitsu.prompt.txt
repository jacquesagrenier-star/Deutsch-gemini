A real photograph of a small evening Japanese class for foreign residents in a neighbourhood community centre in Tokyo in June, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a student at the back of the room would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at seated eye height from the back corner of the room, looking past the students' desks towards the teacher and the blackboard, everything in sharp focus. Warm white ceiling light and the blue light of dusk in the windows; the textures of real laminated wood, painted steel, paper, chalk dust, cotton cloth, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt and the black jeans, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the blackboard is plain dark green with a few soft smudges of white chalk dust, the books have plain single-colour covers, the notebooks are open on blank pages, the sheets of paper are blank white, the walls are plain pale cream, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare desk, bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE STUDENTS, across the lower half of the picture:
- three rows of small wooden desks, two desks to a row, with grey steel chairs;
- in the front row on the left, a Brazilian man of about thirty with short curly black hair and a short beard, in a green shirt, writing in his notebook with a pencil, a thick dictionary open beside him;
- in the front row on the right, a Vietnamese woman of about twenty-five with long straight black hair tied back, in a white blouse, turning round to look at the young woman behind her with a surprised smile;
- in the middle row on the right, the young woman of the reference pictures, seen from her side in three-quarter view from behind, her face in profile turned towards the teacher; she has one hand raised high in the air, her mouth open as she speaks, eager and sure of herself; on her desk, her notebook with a plain red cover, a black pen and a book with a plain blue cover; her olive-green canvas bag hangs on the back of her chair;
- in the middle row on the left, an older Korean woman with short grey hair and glasses, in a lilac cardigan, holding a blank sheet of paper, looking at the young woman with raised eyebrows.

THE FRONT OF THE ROOM, across the upper part of the picture:
- the wide dark-green blackboard covers the front wall, plain, with soft chalk smudges;
- in front of it stands the teacher, a Japanese man of about forty-five with short black hair and black-rimmed glasses, in a white shirt with rolled sleeves and a dark tie, holding a stick of white chalk in one hand; he has turned from the board towards the young woman, his eyebrows raised, half amused;
- the teacher's desk stands beside him, with a stack of books, a grey box of chalk and a glass of water;
- on the left wall, two tall windows with plain white curtains drawn to the sides, the blue evening sky and the roofs of the town beyond them;
- on the right wall, a bookshelf of plain-coloured books and a round white wall clock;
- the ceiling is white with two long white lights.

The classroom is small and friendly: between the objects and the people, the same bare wooden desks, the same bare floor and the same plain pale-cream walls.
