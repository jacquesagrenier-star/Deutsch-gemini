A real photograph of the inside of a modern German regional train on a sunny spring afternoon, taken as a passenger sitting across the aisle would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, bright daylight coming through the large window. The textures of real fabric, plastic, glass, metal, paper, leather and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a blue denim jacket over a white T-shirt and dark jeans.

Every object in the train stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: no signs, no screens with text, no labels, no logos, and the newspaper has plain blank pages with only a photo of a forest on them.

THE SEATS fill the middle of the picture: two pairs of blue fabric seats facing each other, with a small grey folding table between them, under a large window.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures sits in the window seat on the right, facing left towards the window, seen from his head down to the table, which hides him below the waist. He is caught in the middle of the journey: he holds a white paper coffee cup with a plastic lid in one hand on the table, and he looks out of the window at the landscape with a calm half-smile that reaches his eyes.

ON THE TABLE, from the left of the picture to the right:

- a folded newspaper;
- a bread roll on a paper napkin;
- the young man's phone, lying face down;
- a train ticket: a plain pink card with no writing.

AROUND THE SEATS:

- on the left, the sliding glass door of the carriage, closed;
- a round clock with a plain white face and no numbers, high on the wall at the end of the carriage.

THROUGH THE LARGE WINDOW, the German countryside in spring, from near to far:

- a village with red roofs and a white church with a pointed steeple;
