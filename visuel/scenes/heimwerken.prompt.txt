A real photograph of a bright, half-empty living room in an old Berlin flat on a Saturday afternoon, just after moving in, taken as a friend standing near the door would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, daylight from a tall window on the right. The textures of real wood, metal, cardboard, paint, plaster, fabric, plastic and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are an old grey T-shirt with a few white paint spots, faded blue jeans and grey sneakers.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the boxes are plain brown cardboard, the instruction sheet shows only simple drawings with no words, the paint tin has no label, and nothing carries a logo.

THE FLOOR, the lower part of the picture, is old wooden floorboards:
- an open red metal toolbox with a hammer, a pair of pliers and a screwdriver lying next to it;
- a yellow electric drill lying on the floor;
- a small heap of screws and nails on a white saucer;
- a yellow folding wooden ruler, unfolded;
- a large flat cardboard box, open, with long white wooden boards inside;
- a big white sheet of instructions with simple drawings, lying on the floor;
- a pair of work gloves.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures kneels on the floor, seen whole from his head to his feet, with his shadow on the floorboards beside him. He is caught in the middle of building a white bookshelf: he holds a white board upright with one hand and a screwdriver in the other, and looks at the drawing of the instructions with a puzzled, amused frown.

THE ROOM behind him, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a white bookshelf already half built, standing against the wall, still empty;
- a wooden ladder standing open next to the wall;
- on the wall, a large patch freshly painted light blue, with an open tin of blue paint and a wide brush resting on its rim on the floor beneath it;
- a white power socket low on the wall, with a black cable plugged into it;
- a bare light bulb hanging from the ceiling on its wire;
- a stack of three closed cardboard boxes;
- an old wooden wardrobe;
- the tall window with white frames and the green leaves of a tree outside;
- a grey cat sitting on the windowsill, watching him.
