A real photograph of a narrow lane in an old part of Tokyo on a rainy afternoon in June, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a neighbour under the eaves would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height, looking along the lane past a small coin laundry, everything in sharp focus. Soft grey daylight and steady rain; the textures of real wet asphalt, puddles, old wood, grey roof tiles, plaster, clear plastic, raindrops, leaves, petals, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build, her height and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them. Here her curls are a little frizzy from the damp.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are plain grey and cream plaster and bare wood, the front of the laundry is clear glass in a plain white frame, the washing machines are plain white with round glass doors, the umbrellas are plain single colours, and the bags are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare asphalt, bare wall or bare glass around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the wet asphalt of the lane, shining, with two round puddles in which the rain makes small rings;
- on the left, a large bush of blue and pink hydrangea flowers in front of a low wooden fence, raindrops on the leaves;
- on the right, under the eaves of the laundry, a grey tabby cat sits dry on a wooden step, watching the rain.

THE MIDDLE OF THE PICTURE:
- in the middle of the lane, the young woman of the reference pictures walks towards the camera, seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which are splashing in the shallow water with their reflection under them; she holds a small clear plastic umbrella over her head with one hand, tilted against the rain, and with the other hand she hugs a big plain white cloth bag full of laundry against her side; her face is turned a little up towards the rain, laughing at the downpour;
- on the right of her, the front of the coin laundry: a wide glass front, and inside, a row of four plain white washing machines with round glass doors, one turning with clothes inside, and a plain wooden bench with a folded newspaper on it;
- two bicycles stand parked against the wall of the laundry, wet, one with a basket on the front.

THE BACK OF THE LANE, across the upper third of the picture:
- further along the lane, a small boy in a yellow rain hat and yellow boots walks holding the hand of his mother, who holds a plain dark-blue umbrella over them both;
- an old man in a grey jacket holds a plain red umbrella and walks away from the camera;
- on both sides, old two-storey wooden houses with grey tiled roofs, their windows closed, rain running off the edges of the roofs;
- a tall green tree stands over the lane;
- above, a strip of pale grey sky, full of rain.

The lane is quiet and wet: between the objects and the people, the same bare wet asphalt, the same bare plaster walls and the same plain grey roofs.
