A real photograph of the counter area of a small neighbourhood post office in Berlin on a weekday morning, taken as a person waiting in the queue a few metres away would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, daylight from a large window on the left and white ceiling lights. The textures of real wood, cardboard, paper, metal, glass, plastic, fabric and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a camel-coloured wool coat over a white sweater, dark jeans and brown ankle boots.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the parcels are plain brown cardboard tied with string, the envelopes are plain white with no address written on them, the stamps are small plain coloured squares, the screens are dark, and nothing carries a sign, a logo or a brand.

THE COUNTER fills the middle of the picture: a long light-wood counter seen from slightly to the side. On it, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a flat silver parcel scale with a brown parcel on it;
- a small stack of plain white envelopes;
- a sheet of small plain coloured stamps, red and blue;
- a black card payment terminal;
- a pen on a little chain;
- a small green potted plant.

THE CLERK, a man of about forty with short black hair and a neat black beard, in a plain dark-yellow polo shirt, stands behind the counter and smiles at the young woman while he weighs her parcel.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands in front of the counter, seen whole from her head to her feet, which stand on the grey floor with their shadow beside them. She is caught in the middle of handing over a letter: she holds out a white envelope towards the clerk with one hand and holds a second small parcel under her other arm, with a friendly smile. A brown leather handbag hangs from her shoulder.

THE ROOM around them, from the left of the picture to the right:
- the large window with the grey street outside and a yellow postbox standing on the pavement;
- a wooden shelf on the wall with plain brown boxes of different sizes stacked on it;
- behind the clerk, a wall of small wooden pigeon-holes with a few letters in them;
- a round wall clock with a plain white face and no numbers;
- on the right, an old woman with white hair and a green coat waits her turn, holding a small parcel;
- a wooden bench along the right wall with a black umbrella leaning against it;
- in the foreground on the floor, a large brown parcel and a grey plastic basket full of letters.
