A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the inside of a small neighbourhood pharmacy in Berlin on a grey weekday morning, taken as a customer waiting by the door a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, white ceiling lights and grey daylight from the shop window on the right. The textures of real cardboard, glass, plastic, steel, paper, fabric and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a dark-blue padded jacket open over a grey hoodie, blue jeans, white sneakers and a red knitted scarf.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the medicine boxes are plain white and pale-blue cardboard boxes with no print, the bottles and jars are plain with no labels, the health insurance card is a plain blue plastic card, the prescription is a plain pink sheet of paper, the computer screen is dark, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, and the shop has no cross, no sign, no logo and no brand anywhere.

THE COUNTER runs across the middle of the picture, a white counter seen from slightly to the side. On the counter top, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a dark computer screen with a keyboard, turned towards the pharmacist;
- a small black cash register;
- a white paper bag standing open;
- a plain white box of tablets and a small brown glass bottle of syrup with no label, already set out for the young man;
- a pack of paper tissues;
- a small green plant in a pot at the end of the counter.

THE PHARMACIST, a woman of about forty-five with short dark hair and glasses, in a plain white work jacket over a blue blouse, stands behind the counter and takes the prescription from the young man with one hand, looking at him with a kind smile.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures stands in front of the counter, seen whole from his head to his sneakers, which stand on the pale grey floor with their shadow beside them. He has a cold and is caught in the middle of handing over his prescription: he holds the pink sheet out to the pharmacist with one hand, and with the other he presses a white paper tissue to his nose, his eyes a little tired. His blue health insurance card lies on the counter in front of him.
