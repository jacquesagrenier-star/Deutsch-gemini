A real photograph of the arrivals hall of a Japanese international airport on a bright spring afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a traveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the middle of the hall, looking across it towards the tall glass wall and the airfield beyond, everything in sharp focus. Bright daylight from the glass wall and soft white ceiling lights; the textures of real polished stone floor, brushed steel, glass, hard plastic, paper, corduroy, cotton, leather and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build, her height and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens are dark, the two boards hanging from the ceiling are plain blue rectangles, the paper map is pale green and pale blue crossed by thin coloured lines, the suitcases and bags are plain single colours, the airplane is plain white with one plain blue stripe, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young woman of the reference pictures stands just left of the middle, seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which stand on the polished pale-grey stone floor with their soft shadow beside them; her body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; she is caught in the middle of reading an unfolded paper map that she holds open in both hands at chest height, her eyes on the map, her eyebrows a little raised, half amused and half lost;
- an old black film camera hangs on a brown leather strap around her neck and rests on her jacket; an olive-green canvas shoulder bag hangs at her hip;
- on the right of her, on the picture's right, her red hard-shell suitcase stands upright on its four wheels, its handle pulled up;
- around her, the bare polished floor reflects the light of the glass wall.

THE MIDDLE OF THE HALL, from left to right:
- on the left, a row of four grey seats joined on a steel bar; an old man in a grey jacket sits on the end seat reading a folded newspaper, a brown travelling bag on the floor beside his seat;
- in the centre, a little further back, a man in a dark suit and tie stands waiting, holding a bunch of yellow flowers in both hands, looking towards the exit doors on the left; a small boy in a yellow cap stands beside him, holding the hem of his jacket;
- on the right, the information counter: a low white counter with the back of a dark screen on it and a small green plant in a white pot at its end; behind it stands a young woman clerk in a navy-blue uniform jacket with a pale-blue scarf at her neck, her hands resting on the counter, open and empty, looking towards the young woman with the map with a polite smile;
- on the white wall above the counter, a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands.

THE BACK OF THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- on the left, the wide sliding glass doors of the arrivals exit stand open, and beside them an escalator with a steel side rises towards an upper floor, with two travellers standing on it, each holding a small bag;
- across the back, the tall glass wall, in large clear panes held by thin steel frames;
- through the glass, on the grey airfield, a plain white airplane stands seen from the side, with one plain blue stripe along its body;
- above the airfield, a pale-blue spring sky with a few soft white clouds.

The ceiling is white panels with rows of round lights, and the two plain blue boards hang from it on thin steel rods. The hall is calm and spacious: between the objects and the people, the same bare polished stone floor and the same bare white walls.
