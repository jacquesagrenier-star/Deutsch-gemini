A real photograph of the same small pastry shop of the reference picture in the evening, during the football derby on television, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height near the door, looking along the counter towards the television high on the wall, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from the round lamps and the flickering light of the television; the textures of real marble, glass, chrome, glazed tiles, scarves, beer glasses, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the place: the same small pastry shop: the blue and white tiled walls, the dark wooden shelves, the long marble counter, the chrome coffee machine, the round white lamps and the clock stay exactly as in it.

The fourth reference picture shows the owner of the pastry shop, the man with the thick white moustache: his face, his thick white moustache, his bald head, his build and his red-and-blue checked shirt stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the scarves are plain red or plain green, the television screen shows only a green football pitch with small players, the glasses are plain, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:

THE MIDDLE OF THE SHOP, from left to right:
- everyone else in the shop wears a plain red scarf and has frozen in silent dismay, turning slowly to look at him: the owner of the fourth reference picture behind the counter with a towel over his shoulder, an old man in a flat cap, two young men with beers.

THE BACK OF THE SHOP, across the upper third of the picture:

The shop is crowded but each person stands clearly on their own: between them, the same bare floor and the same tiled walls.
