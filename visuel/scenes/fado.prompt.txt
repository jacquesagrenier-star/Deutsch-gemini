A real photograph of a small fado house in the Alfama district of Lisbon on a Sunday night, during a song, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a guest would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at sitting eye height from one of the tables, looking across the room towards the singer and the musicians, everything in sharp focus. Dim warm candlelight on the tables and a soft lamp on the singer; the textures of real stone walls, dark wood, candle wax, glass, wine, the wood of guitars, wool, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the hostel receptionist, the young man with the black beard: his face, his black beard, his build and his grey wool cardigan stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tablecloths are plain red and white checks, the bottles are plain dark glass, the walls are plain whitewashed stone, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- a small table with a red and white checked cloth, a candle in a bottle, two glasses of red wine;
- the young man of the reference pictures sits at it on the left, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he leans towards the woman at the next table, his hand beside his mouth, caught in the middle of whispering.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- every other guest at the small tables has turned round towards the young man, each with one finger on the lips; an old lady glares at him over her glasses;
- a waiter has stopped in the middle of the room, a tray in his hand, his finger on his lips too.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- the singer of the third reference picture stands with his eyes closed and a black shawl over his shoulders, singing with all his heart;
- beside him, two musicians on chairs: one plays a round Portuguese guitar with twelve strings, the other a classical guitar;
- the whitewashed stone vault, an old lantern on the wall, and a framed portrait of a woman in a black shawl.

The room is small and quiet: between the tables, the same bare stone floor and the same bare whitewashed walls.
