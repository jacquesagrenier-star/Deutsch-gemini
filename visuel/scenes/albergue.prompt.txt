A real photograph of the reception of a youth hostel in the Alfama district of Lisbon on a sunny October morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a guest would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the hostel entrance, looking across the reception counter towards the back wall and the street door on the right, everything in sharp focus. Morning daylight from a tall open door and warm light from a lamp on the counter; the textures of real painted wood, old stone, glazed tiles, paper, metal keys, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the big city map on the wall is pale green and pale blue crossed by thin coloured lines with no words, the keys hang on plain wooden pegs, the backpacks are plain single colours, the screen is dark, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the wooden reception counter crosses the bottom of the picture, with a bell, a small lamp, a pile of folded towels and a jar of pens on it;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands behind the counter, on the left of the middle, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he points with great confidence to the right, towards the open street door, his chin up, very sure of himself.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- on the left, behind the young man, his colleague, a young man of twenty-nine with a thick black beard and a grey wool cardigan, waves both arms wildly in the opposite direction, to the left, his face in comic despair;
- on the right, three young travellers with big backpacks walk obediently out of the open door, following the direction the young man shows;
- a wooden bench with a guitar case lying on it.

THE BACK WALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- a big city map of Lisbon on the wall, with a red pin on the castle at the top left;
- a board of wooden pegs where room keys hang;
- a band of blue and white glazed tiles along the wall, and a ceiling fan above.

The reception is cheerful and uncluttered: between the people, the same bare stone floor and the same bare white walls.
