A real photograph of a small old corner grocery shop in the Graça district of Lisbon on a September evening, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a customer would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the shop door, looking towards the counter at the back, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from a bare bulb above the counter and the blue evening light from the door; the textures of real wood, cardboard, tin, glass, dried fish, fruit skins, paper, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tins are plain colours with blank sides, the crates are plain unpainted wood, the fruit has plain unmarked skins, the paper bags are plain brown, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tiled floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- wooden crates of fruit and vegetables line the bottom of the picture: oranges, tomatoes, potatoes, onions and lettuces with plain unmarked skins;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands in the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds in both arms a whole dried salt cod, stiff and flat as a wooden board, taller than he is, its tail above his head, and looks at it in disbelief;
- on the right, a little grey cat sits on a sack of potatoes and watches him.

THE MIDDLE OF THE SHOP, from left to right:
- on the left, wooden shelves with tins of sardines in rows, bottles of olive oil and packets of rice;
- in the centre, the old wooden counter with an old scale on it and a basket of eggs;
- behind the counter, the grocer, a tall thin man of sixty in a grey work coat, holds out both hands as if to say: it's a whole one, what did you expect, with a big grin.

THE BACK OF THE SHOP, across the upper third of the picture:
- from a wooden beam above the counter hang three more whole dried salt cods and a bunch of garlic;
- high shelves with bottles of wine and a calendar with a plain picture of the sea;
- a bare light bulb hangs from the ceiling on its cord.

The shop is small and full but tidy: between the things, the same bare tiled floor and the same bare wooden shelves.
