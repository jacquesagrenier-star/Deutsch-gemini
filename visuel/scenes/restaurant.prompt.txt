A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the dining room of a small neighbourhood restaurant in Berlin on an autumn evening, taken as a guest standing by the entrance a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm light from the hanging lamps and the candles, the blue dusk in the window at the back. The textures of real dark wood, white linen, glass, ceramic, brass, paper, fabric and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a dark-green sweater over a white shirt whose collar shows, dark jeans and brown leather shoes.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the menu is a plain folded card of thick cream paper with no print, the waitress's notepad shows only blank paper, the bottles on the bar are plain dark glass with no labels, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, the picture on the wall is a plain landscape painting of a lake, and nothing in the room carries a sign, a logo, a brand or a chalkboard.
