A real photograph of the great riverside square of Lisbon at midnight on New Year's Eve, under the fireworks, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as someone in the crowd would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in the crowd, looking across the square towards the triumphal arch and the river, everything in sharp focus. The bursting colours of fireworks and the golden light of the arcades; the textures of real stone paving, glass, paper, wool coats, scarves, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the landlady, the small round old woman with short white hair: her face, her short white hair, her build and her apron printed with large red flowers stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the little bags of raisins are plain paper, the coats are plain colours, the bottles are plain glass, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare paving around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle, a dark-grey wool coat open over his sweatshirt, seen from his head down to his knees; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; his cheeks are puffed out like a hamster's, full of raisins, and he tries to smile and speak at the same time, a little paper bag of raisins in his hand;
- beside him on the right, the landlady of the third reference picture, in a black coat and a red scarf, eats her raisins one by one very calmly, counting on her fingers.

THE MIDDLE OF THE SQUARE, from left to right:
- the crowd: people in coats raise glasses of sparkling wine, a couple kisses, a father carries a small girl on his shoulders;
- a young man in a woolly hat holds up a phone to film the fireworks.

THE BACK, across the upper third of the picture:
- the great triumphal arch of the square and its yellow arcades;
- above the river, fireworks burst in gold, red and green in the black sky;
- the dark river with the lights of the bridge in the distance.

The square is full and joyful, but the people in front stand clearly on their own, with bare paving around their feet.
