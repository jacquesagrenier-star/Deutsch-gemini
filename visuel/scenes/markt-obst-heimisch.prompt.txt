A photorealistic photograph of a fruit stall at an outdoor weekly market in Berlin on a bright, slightly overcast morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens. Taken with a 35 mm lens at a customer's eye height, looking down a little onto the stall, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from behind, the second from the front: her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are her everyday clothes.

THE STALL fills the picture from the left edge to the right edge and from just below the top edge down to the bottom edge. It is a long sloping table under a plain dark-green awning, with the fruit displayed in sixteen open shallow crates of plain raw wood, four crates side by side in each of four rows that climb towards the back, every crate tilted towards the camera so that its fruit is fully visible. Each crate holds ONE kind of fruit, heaped and generous, and the fruit itself is the only thing that tells the crates apart: the crates are bare wood, the awning is plain green cloth, and every fruit shows its own bare skin.

The crates, row by row, from the back row at the top of the picture to the front row at the bottom, each row from left to right:
- back row: a crate of punnets of red raspberries; a crate of punnets of dark-blue blueberries; a crate of bunches of green grapes; a crate of dark-blue plums.
- second row: a crate of bundles of pink-red rhubarb stalks; a crate of walnuts in their shells; a crate of green pears; a crate of punnets of bright red currants on their stems.
- third row: a crate of punnets of pale-green gooseberries; a crate of dark-red cherries with their stalks; a crate of punnets of glossy black blackberries; a crate of round orange apricots.
- front row: a crate of punnets of strawberries; a crate of fuzzy pink-and-yellow peaches; a crate of shiny red apples with unmarked skins, at the right-hand end of the row; and one more crate of red apples behind Anna, mostly hidden by her.

ANNA, the young woman of the reference pictures, stands at the stall in the lower right part of the picture, seen from behind and cropped by the right and bottom edges of the picture: we see her head, her ponytail, her shoulders and her back down to the waist, in a simple oatmeal knitted sweater. She leans slightly towards the front row, and her hand nearer the stall reaches into the crate of red apples and holds one apple. She covers only the right-hand end of the front two rows; every other crate stays fully visible.
