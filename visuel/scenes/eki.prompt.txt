A real photograph of a small suburban railway station in Japan on a bright overcast morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a traveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens from the entrance hall at standing eye height, looking straight through the ticket gates onto the platform and the waiting train beyond them, everything in sharp focus. Soft daylight under the platform roof; the textures of real concrete, brushed steel, painted metal, rubber, glass, fabric and skin.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens of the machines and of the train are dark, the signboards are plain blue rectangles and plain white rectangles, the platform number is a plain white disc on a blue board, the posters are plain pale-grey rectangles, and the train's bodywork carries a single plain coloured stripe. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor, bare wall or bare platform around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE ENTRANCE HALL, along the bottom of the picture:
- across the lower middle, a row of three automatic ticket gates of brushed steel, their plastic flaps standing open, each gate with a plain dark card reader on its top and a green light; the gates run from left to right and the camera looks straight through the middle lane;
- at the left edge, a ticket vending machine fixed to the wall, a steel box with a dark blank screen, a coin slot, a note slot and a plain grey map board above it;
- at the right edge, the station clerk's window, a booth with a glass front, and inside it a station clerk in a dark-blue uniform jacket and a dark-blue peaked cap, standing, looking out at the hall; his hands rest on the sill of his window, open and empty;
- the floor of the hall is pale grey tiles.

THE PLATFORM, across the middle of the picture, beyond the gates:
- a line of raised yellow tactile tiles runs along the edge of the platform from the left edge of the picture to the right edge, a plain bright-yellow strip of the same width all the way across;
- on the platform, from left to right: a dark-green metal bench with wooden slats, with an old woman in a grey coat sitting on it, her blue rolling suitcase standing upright beside the bench, its handle pulled up; a tall drinks vending machine with a lit front showing rows of plain coloured bottles and cans behind glass; a square grey rubbish bin with a round opening; a square concrete pillar painted white, holding up the roof;
- two people stand waiting behind the yellow strip: a businessman in a dark suit holding a black briefcase, seen from behind, and a schoolgirl in a navy sailor uniform with a red backpack, seen in profile, looking towards the train; their shoes stand on the concrete platform, with soft shadows under them;
- a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands hangs from the underside of the roof above the middle of the platform;
- at the far right of the platform, a flight of concrete stairs with a steel handrail goes up to a footbridge.

THE TRAIN, across the upper part of the picture:
- a silver commuter train stands at the platform, seen from the side, its plain silver body crossed by one plain orange stripe, its sliding doors open on the platform, its windows showing the bright empty interior with its blue seats, and its destination display above the doors dark;
- the train stands on its rails, and a strip of gravel and the far platform's edge show under it;
- above the train, the underside of the platform roof with its steel beams, and at the very top, a strip of pale overcast sky.

The station is clean and quiet: between the objects and the people, the same bare grey tiles, the same bare concrete platform and the same plain white walls.
