A real photograph of the tatami living room of an old wooden family house in Kanazawa on a hot August afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a relative standing in the doorway would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the sliding door of the room, looking down a little at a family kneeling round a low table and out through the open veranda to the garden, everything in sharp focus. Soft summer daylight from the garden; the textures of real woven straw, dark polished wood, rice paper, lacquer, ceramic, cotton, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her grandmother Fumiko, a Japanese woman of eighty-two: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Fumiko's face, short white hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain white blouse and a plain dark-navy cotton skirt, and Fumiko a plain grey summer cardigan over a plain dark-blue dress.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are plain sand-coloured plaster and dark wood, the sliding doors are plain cream paper, the photograph on the family altar shows a smiling old man against a plain grey background, the cushions are plain dark-blue cloth, the cups are plain white, and the tray of sweets is plain red lacquer. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tatami, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FAMILY, across the lower half of the picture:
- the floor is pale-green tatami mats with dark cloth borders;
- in the middle, a low rectangular table of dark polished wood; on it, a small brown clay teapot, five white tea cups, a red lacquer tray of small pastel sweets, and a plate of sliced watermelon;
- around the table, flat square dark-blue cushions on the tatami;
- at the far side of the table kneels Fumiko of the third reference picture, upright, her hands resting on her knees, smiling gently at her granddaughter, her eyes shining;
- beside her kneels her daughter Keiko, a Japanese woman of about fifty-five with short black hair and round tortoiseshell glasses, in a plain lilac blouse, also upright, holding back a laugh;
- at the end of the table sits a boy of eleven with short black hair, in a plain blue baseball shirt, cross-legged, staring at the young woman with his mouth a little open;
- in the foreground, on the near side of the table, the young woman of the reference pictures kneels on a cushion, seen from her side, bowing very deeply, her forehead almost touching the tatami and her hands flat on the mat in front of her, her curly hair falling forward; the whole family, already upright, waits for her to rise;
- her red hard-shell suitcase and the gift box in cream paper with a red cord stand on the tatami by the door behind her.

THE ROOM, across the middle of the picture:
- against the left wall, a tall family altar of dark lacquered wood with its little doors open; inside it, the framed photograph of the smiling old man, a small brass bowl, two thin white candles, a small vase of white flowers and a dish of fruit: two peaches and a bunch of green grapes;
- on the right, an old electric fan with a round white grille on a stand, turning slowly;
- a round wall clock with a plain white face above the altar.

THE GARDEN, across the upper third of the picture:
- the glass sliding doors of the veranda stand wide open onto a narrow wooden veranda of polished boards;
- beyond it, a small garden: a stone lantern, a twisted pine tree, a few round stones in raked gravel, green moss, and a tall tree with dark-green leaves against a pale summer sky;
- a small glass wind chime hangs from the eaves above the veranda.

The house is old, cool and welcoming: between the objects and the people, the same bare tatami, the same bare dark wood and the same plain sand-coloured walls.
