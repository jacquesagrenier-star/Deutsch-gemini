A real photograph of a narrow sunny lane of the Graça district of Lisbon seen from a third-floor window on an October Saturday, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a neighbour would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at the height of a third-floor window, looking down into the lane and across at the facades opposite, everything in sharp focus. Bright midday sunlight and sharp shadows across the lane; the textures of real glazed tiles, painted plaster, wrought iron, cotton laundry, wooden pegs, cobblestones and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the landlady, the small round old woman with short white hair: her face, her short white hair, her build and her apron printed with large red flowers stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the laundry is plain single-coloured cloth, the umbrella is plain black, the tiles of the facades are plain green and white squares, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wall or bare cobblestones around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the window sill of the young man's room crosses the bottom of the picture, with a laundry line stretched from it on two pulleys across the lane, laden with shirts, towels and socks held by wooden pegs;
- the young man of the reference pictures leans out of his window on the left, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; one hand holds a wooden peg in the air, the other hand reaches down too late, his face frozen in horror.

THE LANE BELOW, from left to right:
- far below in the cobbled lane, a bald man in a cardigan walks under a big black umbrella against the sun; on top of his umbrella lies one royal-blue sock; he holds the sock up between two fingers at arm's length and looks up at the window with comic dignity;
- a small white dog on a lead stops to sniff the air;
- a scooter is parked against the wall.

THE FACADES, across the upper third of the picture:
- the facade opposite is covered in plain green and white tiles, with windows with green shutters and wrought-iron balconies full of flower pots with red geraniums;
- at the window directly below the young man's, the landlady of the third reference picture leans out and laughs, her hand over her mouth, a yellow canary in its white cage hanging beside her window;
- above the roofs, a strip of deep blue sky and a seagull.

The lane is narrow and sunny: between the facades, the same bare cobblestones and the same strip of sky.
