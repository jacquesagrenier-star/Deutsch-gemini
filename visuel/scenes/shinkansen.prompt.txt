A real photograph of the inside of a Japanese high-speed train on a sunny August morning, on its way across the countryside, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a passenger standing in the aisle would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the aisle, looking down a little at a young woman in a window seat and out through the wide window at the fields and mountains, everything in sharp focus. Bright summer daylight through the window; the textures of real woven seat fabric, moulded plastic, glass, paper, lacquered wood, cotton, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her clothes, the white t-shirt and the black jeans, stay exactly as in them; her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket lies folded on her knees.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the seats are plain dark-blue fabric with plain white headrest covers, the lunch box is plain black lacquer with a plain red lid, the bottle of tea is plain green, the notebook has a plain red cover and lies open on a blank page, the gift box is wrapped in plain cream paper tied with a plain red cord, and the walls of the carriage are plain pale grey. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tray, bare seat or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE SEATS, across the lower half of the picture:
- two rows of dark-blue seats seen from the aisle; in the window seat of the near row sits the young woman of the reference pictures, seen from her side in three-quarter view, her face turned towards her notebook; she holds a black pen above the open page and moves her lips as if repeating something to herself, frowning in concentration, her eyes darting sideways towards her neighbour;
- in front of her, the fold-down tray of the seat ahead is open; on it, the open black lunch box with its red lid set beside it, showing white rice, a piece of grilled fish, a rolled omelette and pickled vegetables, a pair of wooden chopsticks laid across it, the green bottle of tea and her open notebook;
- her olive-green canvas bag stands on the floor at her feet, and her old black film camera rests on the seat beside her;
- in the seat beside her, nearer the aisle, an old man in a pale grey summer shirt, a folded newspaper on his knee, sits upright and turns his head towards her notebook with one forefinger raised, whispering the next words to her, with a mischievous, delighted smile.

THE WINDOW, across the middle of the picture:
- the wide window beside her, with its plain grey blind rolled up;
- through the window, the summer countryside rushing past: bright green rice fields in neat squares, a narrow river with a small bridge, a village of houses with grey and blue tiled roofs, a line of green trees, and far away a range of blue mountains;
- a few white clouds stand in a deep blue sky above the mountains.

THE TOP OF THE PICTURE:
- above the seats, the long luggage rack runs along the wall of the carriage; on it lie her red hard-shell suitcase and the gift box in cream paper with its red cord, and further along, a black travelling bag of another passenger;
- the ceiling of the carriage is plain white with a long strip of soft light.

The carriage is quiet and bright: between the objects and the people, the same bare seats, the same bare window frame and the same plain pale-grey walls.
