A photorealistic photograph of a vegetable stall at an outdoor weekly market in Berlin on a bright, slightly overcast morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens. Taken with a 35 mm lens at a customer's eye height, looking down a little onto the stall, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from behind, the second from the front: her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are her everyday clothes.

THE STALL fills the picture from the left edge to the right edge and from just below the top edge down to the bottom edge. It is a long sloping table under a plain dark-green awning, with the vegetables displayed in open shallow crates of plain raw wood, set side by side in five rows that climb towards the back, every crate tilted towards the camera so that its vegetables are fully visible. Each crate holds ONE kind of vegetable, heaped and generous, and the vegetables themselves are the only thing that tells the crates apart: the crates are bare wood, the awning is plain green cloth.
