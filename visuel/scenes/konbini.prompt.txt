A real photograph of the inside of a small Japanese convenience store late in the evening, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens from just inside the entrance at standing eye height, looking down the length of the store towards the counter at the back, everything in sharp focus. Bright, even white ceiling light; the textures of real glossy floor tiles, steel shelving, clear plastic film, cardboard, glass and skin.

Every surface in the picture is plain: each packet, bottle, can and carton is a single solid colour with a plain band of a second colour, the labels and price tags are blank white cards, the posters on the walls are plain pale-blue rectangles, the screens of the register and of the microwave are dark, and the signs hanging from the ceiling are plain white rectangles. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare shelf, bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom edge of the picture:
- on the left, a stack of red plastic shopping baskets standing on the floor, the top one slightly apart from the others;
- on the right, a round white bucket of clear transparent umbrellas, their curved handles pointing up;
- between them, the pale glossy floor tiles, with the soft reflection of the ceiling lights in them.

THE MIDDLE OF THE STORE, from left to right:
- along the left wall, a tall white steel shelf with rows of goods: on the top shelves packets of bread rolls in clear film, below them bags of snacks and sweets in plain coloured bags, and on the lowest shelf a row of instant-noodle cups with plain white lids;
- in the centre, a low open chilled display case seen from its end, holding on its upper tray a tidy row of triangular rice balls, each wrapped in clear film with a plain band of dark-green seaweed showing through, and on its lower tray a row of lunch boxes with clear lids over white rice, a fried cutlet and a few vegetables, next to a stack of triangle sandwiches in clear film;
- along the right wall, a long row of glass-fronted refrigerators with lit white interiors, their shelves full of bottles and cans in plain colours: clear bottles of water, green bottles of tea, white cartons of milk, small brown cans of coffee and orange bottles of juice; an old man in a grey jacket and a flat cap stands in front of the nearest refrigerator, holding its glass door open with one hand and a green bottle of tea in the other hand, looking at the shelf;
- a round white wall clock with a black rim hangs high on the left wall above the shelf.

THE BACK OF THE STORE, across the upper third of the picture:
- the counter runs across the back, a plain white counter; on it, from left to right, a black cash register with a dark screen, a white microwave oven with a dark window, and a small stack of folded white plastic bags;
- behind the counter stands the cashier, a young man in a plain green-and-white striped shirt with a plain white name badge, his hands resting on the counter, open and empty, looking at the customer in front of him;
- in front of the counter, seen from behind and in three-quarter view, stands a young woman in a beige coat with a dark-blue shoulder bag; her red shopping basket with a few plain packets in it rests on the counter, and she holds a small coin out towards the cashier between her thumb and forefinger; her flat shoes stand on the floor tiles, with a soft shadow under them;
- behind the cashier, on the back wall, a shelf of plain coloured boxes and bottles in a tidy row, and beside it a plain pale-blue poster;
- in the top right corner, the glass entrance door with its steel frame, closed, showing a dark street and the blurred lights of a passing car outside.

The ceiling is white with two long rows of bright fluorescent tubes running towards the back of the store. The store is tidy and well stocked: between the objects and the people, the same bare glossy floor, the same bare white shelving and the same plain walls.
