A real photograph of the entrance hall of an old Lisbon metro station whose walls are covered with blue and white tiles, on a February morning, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a commuter would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in front of the ticket gates, looking across them towards the escalator, everything in sharp focus. Even white light from the ceiling; the textures of real glazed tiles, brushed steel, glass, plastic cards, wool coats, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tiles are plain blue and white with simple waves and fish drawn on them, the cards are plain green, the screens are dark, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- a row of steel ticket gates crosses the bottom of the picture, with glass flaps;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands at the left gate, seen from his head down to his knees; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he presses a green card on the reader for the third time, the glass flaps stay shut, and he looks at the card in disbelief;
- at the next gate, a girl of eight with a school bag taps her card with a big smile and walks straight through the open flaps.

THE MIDDLE OF THE HALL, from left to right:
- a ticket machine against the wall on the left;
- commuters in coats walk towards the escalator; a man with a briefcase, a woman with a coffee.

THE BACK OF THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- the walls covered in blue and white tiles drawn with waves, fish and boats;
- the escalator going down, with people on it;
- a station clock hanging from the ceiling.

The hall is bright and orderly: between the people, the same bare floor and the same tiled walls.
