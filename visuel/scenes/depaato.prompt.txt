A real photograph of the gift floor of a big department store in Tokyo in mid-December, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a shopper beside the counter would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the aisle, looking across a glass counter where a sales assistant is wrapping a gift, towards the floor beyond, everything in sharp focus. Bright, warm shop lighting and gleaming glass; the textures of real polished stone floor, glass, brass, lacquered wood, wrapping paper, ribbon, wool, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair and her build stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; here she wears a plain dark-green wool coat, open, over her white t-shirt, a long plain mustard-yellow knitted scarf around her neck, black jeans and white sneakers.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the wrapping paper is plain cream with a plain dark-red ribbon, the boxes and tins are plain single colours, the paper shopping bags are plain white with plain cord handles, the price cards are blank white cards, and the walls and pillars are plain cream. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare glass, bare counter or bare floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE COUNTER, across the lower half of the picture:
- a long glass display counter with a brass edge; inside it, on plain cream shelves, rows of gift boxes of sweets and biscuits in plain colours, small tins of tea in plain green and plain black, and a few boxes of fine handkerchiefs folded in squares;
- on top of the counter, the sales assistant, a young woman in a plain navy uniform jacket with a white blouse and a small plain navy hat, her hair in a neat bun, folds the cream wrapping paper around a square box with quick precise hands, her eyes on the box, smiling slightly;
- beside the box, a roll of the cream paper, a spool of dark-red ribbon, and a plain white paper shopping bag waiting, open;
- the young woman of the reference pictures stands on the near side of the counter, on the left of the picture, seen from her side in three-quarter view down to her hips, one hand resting flat on the brass edge of the counter; she leans a little closer to watch the wrapping, her eyes wide and her lips parted in admiration; her brown wallet lies on the counter next to her hand; with her other hand she holds up her first purchase of the day at the height of her face, in the middle of the picture: a big plain white shopping bag, with a smaller plain white bag standing inside it, and an even smaller white bag standing inside that one, and on the palm of her hand, lifted out of the smallest bag, one tiny wrapped box the size of a matchbox, with a dark-red ribbon; she looks at it with a puzzled smile.

THE SHOP FLOOR, across the middle of the picture:
- other counters of the gift floor: a counter of neckties laid in fans of plain colours and leather gloves in pairs, a counter of folded umbrellas in plain colours standing in a row, and a counter of watches in a glass case;
- shoppers in winter coats walk between the counters: a businessman in a dark coat carrying two shopping bags, an elegant old woman in a grey fur hat looking at the umbrellas, a mother with a little boy in a red knitted hat;
- a tall vase of white and red flowers stands on a round table in the middle of the aisle.

THE BACK OF THE FLOOR, across the upper third:
- on the right, an escalator with a gleaming steel side rises to the floor above, with people standing on it;
- a tall pillar faced with plain cream stone, and on it a large round clock with a plain white face and two black hands;
- the ceiling is plain cream, with rows of small round lights and a long garland of plain green branches with small white lights hanging along it.

The store is elegant and bustling: between the objects and the people, the same bare polished stone floor, the same clear glass and the same plain cream walls.
