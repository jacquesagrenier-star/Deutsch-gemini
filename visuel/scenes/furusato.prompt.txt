A real photograph of the front gate of an old primary school in a quiet part of Kanazawa on a sunny November afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a passer-by on the pavement would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from across the narrow street, looking at two women standing at the school gate and past them into the schoolyard, everything in sharp focus. Low golden autumn sunlight; the textures of real concrete, stone, painted steel, sand, golden leaves, bark, paper, wool, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her grandmother Fumiko, a Japanese woman of eighty-two: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Fumiko's face, short white hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket over a plain grey knitted sweater, black jeans and white sneakers, and Fumiko a plain grey wool coat and a plain dark-blue knitted hat.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the stone gatepost carries a plain grey stone plate, the school building is plain cream concrete with plain windows, the clock on the building has a plain white face with two black hands, the photo album has a plain dark-brown cloth cover, and the photograph in it is an old black-and-white picture of a row of children standing in front of a gate. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare pavement, bare sand or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE STREET, along the bottom of the picture:
- the narrow grey asphalt street with a white line along its edge, and fallen golden leaves blowing across it;
- on the right, a bicycle parked against the low wall, with a basket on the front.

THE GATE, across the middle of the picture:
- the school gate: two square gateposts of grey stone, the left one with its plain grey stone plate, and between them a sliding gate of green-painted steel bars, standing open;
- on both sides of the gate, a low wall of grey concrete with a green hedge behind it;
- in front of the left gatepost, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers on the pavement, in three-quarter view, holding a large old photo album open against her side with one arm; she stands exactly like the little boy in the black-and-white photograph, chin up, chest out, her other fist on her hip, with a very solemn face;
- beside her, Fumiko of the third reference picture stands close, one gloved forefinger pointing at one small boy in the old photograph, laughing with her eyes half closed;
- a little to the right, two schoolchildren in yellow caps with big leather school satchels on their backs walk out of the gate, looking curiously at the two women.

THE SCHOOLYARD AND THE SCHOOL, across the upper third of the picture:
- beyond the gate, the wide sandy schoolyard, with a set of iron bars, a tall tree with bright golden leaves, and a few children playing with a red ball;
- at the back of the yard, the three-storey school building of plain cream concrete, with long rows of windows, and a round white clock on its front wall above the main door;
- behind the school, the low blue hills, and a clear pale-blue autumn sky.

The street is calm and golden: between the people and the school, the same bare pavement, the same bare sand of the yard and the same plain cream walls.
