A real photograph of a park in Tokyo under cherry trees in full bloom on a sunny morning in early April, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend standing on the path would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height on a gravel path lined with cherry trees, looking towards a group meeting a newcomer, and beyond them to a pond with a small red bridge, everything in sharp focus. Bright spring sunlight filtered through the pale-pink blossoms; the textures of real gravel, grass, bark, petals, water, hard plastic, paper, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. The young woman's clothes stay as in the reference pictures, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers; Yui wears a plain cream trench coat over a plain pale-blue sweater.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the paper map is pale green and pale blue crossed by thin coloured lines, the suitcase and the backpack are plain single colours, the groundsheet is plain bright blue, the lunch boxes are plain black with clear lids, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare gravel or bare grass around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE MEETING, across the lower half of the picture:
- the wide gravel path, with a few pink petals lying on it;
- in the middle, a young newcomer, a man of about twenty-five with short sandy hair and freckles, in a plain navy windbreaker, jeans and grey sneakers, with a big plain black backpack on his back and a plain blue hard-shell suitcase standing beside him, its handle up; he bows to her from the waist, far too deep and stiff, an unfolded paper map in one hand and a phone in the other, and his big backpack has tipped forward onto the back of his head;
- facing him, on the left, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which stand on the gravel with their shadow beside them, in three-quarter view; she answers with a small, perfect bow, one hand held out towards him with the palm up, inviting him to join them, holding back a laugh with a warm, welcoming smile;
- beside her, the teacher of the community class, a Japanese man of about forty-five with short black hair and black-rimmed glasses, in a beige jacket, stands with his hands behind his back, watching them with an approving smile;
- an old black film camera hangs on a brown leather strap around the young woman's neck.

THE PICNIC, across the middle of the picture:
- on the grass to the right of the path, a plain bright-blue groundsheet spread under a cherry tree, with shoes lined up neatly along its edge; on it, black lunch boxes, green bottles of tea, paper cups and a packet of sweets;
- Yui of the third reference picture sits on the groundsheet, waving at the newcomer with one hand raised, laughing; next to her, the landlady, a Japanese woman of about fifty-six with short brown hair, in a plain beige cardigan, pours tea into a paper cup.

THE BACK OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- the path runs on between the cherry trees towards a calm pond with a small arched wooden bridge painted red;
- an old man walks a small white dog under the trees;
- overhead, the blossoming branches of the cherry trees fill the top of the picture with pale pink against a clear blue sky.

The park is bright, festive and welcoming: between the objects and the people, the same bare gravel path, the same bare grass and the same blue sky.
