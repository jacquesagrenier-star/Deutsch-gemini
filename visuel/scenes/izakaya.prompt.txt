A real photograph of a small, busy Japanese pub in an old part of Tokyo on a Friday evening in November, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a colleague at the table would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at seated eye height from one end of a low table on a raised tatami floor, looking along the table at a group of colleagues and past them towards the counter and the open kitchen, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from paper lamps and the glow of the grill; the textures of real dark wood, woven straw, glass, beer foam, ceramic, smoke, cotton cloth, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven, the fourth shows the café owner Mr Sakamoto, a Japanese man of sixty-three: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, and Mr Sakamoto's face, cropped grey hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket over a plain grey sweater, Yui a plain cream sweater, and Mr Sakamoto a plain dark-blue shirt.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are bare dark wood and plain cream plaster, the wooden boards on the wall are bare wood, the paper lamps are plain white and plain red, the glasses are clear, the bottles are plain brown and plain green, the plates and bowls are plain white and plain dark blue, and the cushions are plain dark-blue cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare table, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE TABLE, across the lower half of the picture:
- a long low wooden table on the tatami, crowded with food: a plate of grilled chicken skewers, a plate of a grilled fish with a wedge of lemon, a bowl of green soy beans in their pods, a rolled yellow omelette on a dark-blue plate, a small iron pot of stew, small dishes of pickles, pairs of chopsticks, a small white bottle of sake with tiny cups, and a glass ashtray, clean and empty;
- along the far side of the table, sitting on dark-blue cushions: Mr Sakamoto of the fourth reference picture, in the middle, holding up his glass with both hands, a big rare smile on his face; beside him Yui of the third reference picture, laughing, her hand over her mouth; and at the end, the young cook Kenta, a Japanese man of twenty-two with spiky black hair and a plain white towel tied around his head, raising his glass;
- on the near side, the young woman of the reference pictures kneels upright on her cushion, seen from her side in three-quarter view, holding a large brown bottle of beer in both hands, one hand under the bottle and one on its neck, and carefully pouring beer into Mr Sakamoto's glass, concentrating, a proud little smile on her lips; her own glass, still empty, stands in front of her.

THE COUNTER, across the middle of the picture:
- behind the table, the long wooden counter with tall wooden stools; two men in suits sit at it, eating, seen from behind;
- behind the counter, the cook of the pub, a man of about sixty in a plain dark-blue jacket and a plain dark-blue cap, turns skewers on a long charcoal grill, a little smoke rising.

THE BACK OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- on the back wall, shelves of brown and green bottles, and a row of bare wooden boards hanging on hooks;
- a round wall clock with a plain white face;
- plain red and plain white paper lamps hang from the dark wooden ceiling beams, lit, glowing warm.

The pub is crowded, noisy and friendly: between the objects and the people, the same bare dark wood, the same bare tatami and the same plain cream walls.
