A real photograph of a long sandy beach on the Atlantic south of Lisbon on a sunny July morning, during a surf lesson, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend on the beach would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height at the edge of the water, looking out to sea, everything in sharp focus. Bright summer sunlight and sparkling foam; the textures of real wet sand, foam, surfboards, wetsuits, skin and sun.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build and his height stay exactly as in them; it is summer, and he wears a black wetsuit.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the surfboards are plain white or plain yellow, the wetsuits are plain black, the umbrellas are plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare sand or bare water around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the wet sand and the edge of the foam;
- the surf teacher, a sunburnt woman with a whistle around her neck, stands in the shallow water and holds her forehead in her hand.

THE MIDDLE OF THE PICTURE, the waves, from left to right:
- four students stand proudly upright on their white boards, riding a small wave;
- in the middle of them, the young man of the reference pictures, in a black wetsuit, lies flat on his stomach on a yellow board, his arms around it, and waves at the camera with one hand, grinning.

THE BACK, across the upper third of the picture:
- the blue Atlantic and more small waves;
- a red and white lifeguard tower on the beach on the right, with a flag;
- a clear blue sky with a few seagulls.

The beach is wide and open: between the people, the same bare water and the same bare sand.
