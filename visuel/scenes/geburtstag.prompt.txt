A real photograph of the living room of a shared flat in an old Berlin building on a Saturday evening, during a small birthday party, taken as a guest sitting in an armchair would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm light from a floor lamp and a string of small round lights along the window. The textures of real wood, fabric, paper, glass, cake, balloons and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them; his clothes here are a dark-blue shirt with the sleeves rolled up and dark jeans. The third reference picture shows the young woman: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in it; her clothes here are a yellow dress and a white cardigan. Use the reference pictures for the people only.

Every object in the room stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the cards are plain, the wrapping paper is plain or striped, the balloons are plain single colours, the bottles carry no label.

THE TABLE fills the lower half of the picture: a low rectangular wooden table seen from slightly above. On it, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a round chocolate birthday cake on a white plate, with five lit candles on top;
- a stack of small white plates, a few forks and a pile of paper napkins;
- a large pizza in its open plain brown box;
- a white bowl of red strawberries;
- a glass jug of orange juice, two bottles of water with no label, and five glasses;
- two wrapped presents: a large one in plain red paper with a gold ribbon and bow, and a small one in blue-and-white striped paper;
- a plain white greeting card standing open, with only a drawn red heart on it.

THE PEOPLE are caught in the middle of the moment, their eyes on one another and on the present, from the left of the picture to the right:
- the young man of the reference pictures sits in the middle of a grey sofa behind the table, seen from his head down to the table top, which hides him below the waist. He is laughing, both hands raised a little, palms open in happy surprise, his eyes on the present held out to him;
- the young woman of the reference pictures stands to the right of the table, seen from her head to her knees, the edge of the picture crossing her just below the hem of her dress. She stands upright and only her arms reach forward across the corner of the table: with both hands she holds out a small flat present wrapped in green paper with a white ribbon towards him, her eyes on his face, smiling;
- on the left, a young man with short black hair and a beard, in a grey hoodie, sits on the arm of the sofa and holds up his phone, turned towards the young man in the middle, to take a photo of him;
- on the right of the sofa, a young woman with long curly red hair, in a black top, sits on the sofa holding a glass of juice and clapping her free hand against it.

THE ROOM behind them:
- a bunch of balloons, red, yellow and blue, tied to the left end of the sofa, floating above it on their strings;
- an acoustic guitar leaning against the right end of the sofa;
- on the wall above the sofa, a large framed picture of a blue sea and a white boat, and a round wall clock;
- on the left, a tall window with a white frame and the dark evening outside, with a string of small round warm lights along the frame, and a tall green plant in a pot on the floor in front of it;
- on the right, a floor lamp with a white shade, and a wooden bookshelf with books whose spines are plain colours;
- a soft beige rug on the wooden floor under the table.
