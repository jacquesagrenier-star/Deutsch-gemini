A real photograph of a family Sunday lunch in the dining room of an old house in a small German town, taken as a cousin standing at the end of the table would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight from a window at the back. The textures of real wood, linen, china, glass, steel, bread, vegetables, fabric, hair and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a light-grey knitted sweater.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing or print, and nothing carries a logo.

THE TABLE runs from the bottom of the picture to the back, a long wooden table with a white linen tablecloth, seen from its end, slightly from above. On it:
- in the foreground, an empty white plate with a fork on its left, a knife and a spoon on its right and a white folded napkin, for the cousin who takes the picture;
- a big white soup tureen with a ladle, in the middle of the table;
- a large dish of boiled potatoes and a bowl of green salad;
- a basket of sliced bread;
- a glass jug of water and a bottle of orange juice with no label;
- a glass in front of each person;
- a small vase of yellow tulips at the far end.

AROUND THE TABLE, everyone seated, from the front of the picture to the back:
- on the left, THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures, seen from his head down to the table top, which hides him below the waist; he is caught in the middle of laughing at something his grandfather says, holding his spoon;
- on the right, facing him, his sister, a girl of about fifteen with long brown hair and a red sweater, who pours water into her glass;
- further on the left, his mother, a woman of about fifty with short dark-blonde hair and glasses, in a blue blouse, who holds a baby of about one year on her lap;
- further on the right, his father, a man of about fifty-five with grey hair and a moustache, in a checked shirt, who serves potatoes onto his plate;
- at the far end, the grandmother with white hair in a bun and a cardigan, and the grandfather, bald with a white beard and a dark waistcoat, who raises one finger as he tells his story.

THE ROOM at the back:
- a window with white lace curtains and a garden outside;
- a dark wooden sideboard with a few plates standing on it;
- a large framed painting of a landscape on the wall;
- a wall clock with a plain face and no numbers;
- a ginger cat sleeping on a chair in the corner.
