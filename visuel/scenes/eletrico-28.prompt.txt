A real photograph of the inside of an old yellow Lisbon tram climbing a steep cobbled street on a sunny September afternoon, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a passenger would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height near the back door of the tram, looking down the whole length of the aisle in one view, from the passengers close by to the driver far away at the front, everything in sharp focus. Bright afternoon sunlight through the side windows and warm reflections on the varnished wood; the textures of real varnished wood, polished brass, worn leather straps, glass, painted metal, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tram walls are plain varnished wood, the seats are plain wood slats, the bags are plain single colours, the signs are plain cream rectangles, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wooden floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands in the aisle on the left of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds a leather strap hanging from the ceiling with one hand and the handle of his bright grass-green hard-shell suitcase with the other, his eyebrows raised in surprise;
- his suitcase is stuck in the open folding door of the tram on the right of the picture, the door half closed on it;
- a small old lady in a black dress and a black cardigan, a shopping basket on her arm, pushes the suitcase out of the door with a firm bump of her hip, smiling, completely calm.

THE MIDDLE OF THE TRAM, from left to right:
- on the left, wooden seats where a young mother sits with a small boy on her knees; the boy looks at the suitcase, delighted;
- in the centre, two tourists in sun hats hold a paper map between them;
- on the right, a man in a grey suit stands holding a folded newspaper.

THE FAR END OF THE SAME AISLE, across the upper third of the picture:
- at the far end of the aisle, small in the distance and seen through the same wooden interior, the driver in a dark-blue uniform jacket and cap sits at the brass controls with his back to us;
- beyond him, through the front window of the same tram, the steep cobbled street climbs between pale-yellow and white houses, and a church tower stands at the top against a blue sky.

The tram is full but each person stands clearly on their own: between them, the same bare wooden floor and the same bare wooden walls.
