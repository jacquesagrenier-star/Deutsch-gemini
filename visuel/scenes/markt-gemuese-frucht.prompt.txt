A photorealistic photograph of a vegetable stall at an outdoor weekly market in Berlin on a bright, slightly overcast morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens. Taken with a 35 mm lens at a customer's eye height, looking down a little onto the stall, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from behind, the second from the front: her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are her everyday clothes.

THE STALL fills the picture from the left edge to the right edge and from just below the top edge down to the bottom edge. It is a long sloping table under a plain dark-green awning, with the vegetables displayed in fifteen open shallow crates of plain raw wood: four crates side by side in each of the three back rows, and three crates in the front row, the rows climbing towards the back and every crate tilted towards the camera so that its vegetables are fully visible. Each crate holds ONE kind of vegetable, heaped and generous, and the vegetables themselves are the only thing that tells the crates apart: the crates are bare wood, the awning is plain green cloth.

The crates, row by row, from the back row at the top of the picture to the front row at the bottom, each row from left to right:
- back row: a crate of green pea pods; a crate of heads of green lettuce; a crate of bundles of white asparagus; a crate of whole orange pumpkins.
- second row: a crate of red and yellow peppers; a crate of corn cobs in their green husks; a crate of white mushrooms; a crate of green beans.
- third row: a crate of rainbow chard with red and yellow stems; a crate of globe artichokes; a crate of glossy dark-purple aubergines; a crate of long dark-green cucumbers.
- front row: a crate of yellow and green courgettes; a crate of round red tomatoes on the vine, just to the left of Anna; a crate of fresh green spinach leaves, at the right-hand end of the row, partly behind her.

ANNA, the young woman of the reference pictures, stands at the stall in the lower right part of the picture, seen from behind and cropped by the right and bottom edges of the picture: we see her head, her ponytail, her shoulders and her back down to the waist, in a simple oatmeal knitted sweater. She leans slightly towards the front row, and her hand nearer the stall reaches over to the crate of tomatoes just to her left, which stays fully visible, and lifts one vine of tomatoes out of it. Only the last crate of the front row is partly behind her; every other crate stays fully visible.
