A real photograph of a small bright open-plan office in a converted factory building in Berlin on a weekday morning, taken as a colleague standing near the door would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, daylight from tall factory windows on the left and white ceiling lamps. The textures of real wood, metal, plastic, paper, glass, fabric, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a light-blue shirt with the sleeves rolled up and dark chinos.

Every object in the office stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the screens show only a photograph of a lake or a plain blue background, the papers are blank, the folders have plain coloured spines, the whiteboard carries only a few simple drawn boxes and arrows, and nothing carries a logo.

- a black desk phone with its handset;

- an open spiral notebook with blank pages;

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures sits at the desk on a black office chair, turned three-quarters towards the camera, seen from his head down to the desk top, which hides him below the waist. He is caught in the middle of a phone call: he holds the handset of the desk phone to his ear with one hand and writes in the notebook with the pen in his other hand, with a focused half-smile.
