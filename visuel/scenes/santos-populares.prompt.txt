A real photograph of a little square of the Alfama district of Lisbon on the night of the 12th of June, during the street festival of Saint Anthony, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a reveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height at the edge of the square, looking across it towards the grill and the dancers, everything in sharp focus. Colourful paper garlands lit by strings of bulbs, and the orange glow of the grill; the textures of real paper garlands, smoke, grilled sardines, bread, basil plants, cobblestones, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the landlady, the small round old woman with short white hair: her face, her short white hair, her build and her apron printed with large red flowers stay exactly as in it.

The fourth reference picture shows the waitress, the young woman with round red glasses: her face, her dark hair pinned up, her round red glasses and her build stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the paper garlands are plain bright colours, the little flags are plain colours, the paper plates are plain white, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare cobblestones around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle, in his white t-shirt, seen from his head down to his knees; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds a grilled sardine on a slice of bread in one hand, grease dripping, and a small pot of basil in the other, and looks around helplessly for somewhere to put one of them down;
- beside him, the waitress of the fourth reference picture laughs and offers him a paper napkin.

THE MIDDLE OF THE SQUARE, from left to right:
- the landlady of the third reference picture dances joyfully with an old man in a flat cap, her arms raised;
- a man grills sardines on a big smoking charcoal grill;
- long wooden tables with paper plates, jugs of wine and small pots of basil with paper flowers on them.

THE TOP OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- paper garlands and strings of bulbs criss-cross the square between the old houses;
- windows and balconies with people leaning out;
- a dark blue night sky.

The square is full and festive but each person stands clearly on their own: between them, the same bare cobblestones.
