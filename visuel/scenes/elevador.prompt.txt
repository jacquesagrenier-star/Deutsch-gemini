A real photograph of a steep narrow street of Lisbon where a small old yellow funicular climbs on its rails, on a cold February morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a neighbour would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height at the bottom of the street, looking up the rails, everything in sharp focus. Pale winter sunlight between the houses; the textures of real yellow painted metal, steel rails, cobblestones, old plaster, iron balconies, wool, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the funicular is plain yellow with white roof, the facades are plain pastel plaster, the laundry is plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare cobblestones or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the two steel rails run up the middle of the cobbled street from the bottom edge;
- the young man of the reference pictures runs up the street beside the rails on the left of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers, his back half turned to us, his body in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he is out of breath, one arm stretched out towards the funicular.

THE MIDDLE OF THE STREET, from left to right:
- the small yellow funicular car stands still on the rails just ahead of him, its doors open and its passengers seated;
- the driver in a dark-blue uniform leans out of the open door with his chin on his hand, waiting patiently with a bored, amused face;
- a cat sits on a doorstep on the right.

THE TOP OF THE STREET, across the upper third of the picture:
- the narrow street rises steeply between pastel houses with iron balconies and laundry;
- at the very top, a glimpse of the river shining in the sun;
- a pale winter sky.

The street is narrow and steep: between the people, the same bare cobblestones and the same bare walls.
