A real photograph of the first shrine visit of the New Year at a large Shinto shrine in Kanazawa on the morning of the first of January, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a visitor in the crowd would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in the shrine courtyard, looking towards the main hall at the top of a short flight of stone steps, everything in sharp focus. Bright, cold winter sunlight and thin snow on the roofs; the textures of real old wood, grey roof tiles, stone, snow, white paper, straw rope, smoke, wool coats, silk kimono and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her grandmother Fumiko, a Japanese woman of eighty-two, the fourth shows her father Hiroshi, a Japanese man of fifty-eight: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, Fumiko's face, short white hair and build, and Hiroshi's face, short greying hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain dark-green wool coat, a long plain mustard-yellow knitted scarf and plain brown gloves, Fumiko a plain grey wool coat and a plain dark-blue knitted hat, and Hiroshi a plain dark-grey wool coat and a plain grey knitted hat.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the slips of paper are plain white, the wooden boxes are bare wood, the large rope is plain straw, the clothes are plain cloth, and the wooden gate is plain vermilion with plain black bases. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare stone, bare snow or bare wood around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the stone pavement of the courtyard, swept, with a little snow along its edges;
- on the left, a small tree whose bare branches are covered with dozens of slips of white paper folded and tied in knots, like white blossoms;
- the young woman of the reference pictures stands just right of the middle, seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which stand on the stone with their shadow beside them, in three-quarter view; she holds an unfolded slip of white paper open in both gloved hands, reading it, her face bursting into a delighted grin;
- beside her, Hiroshi of the fourth reference picture stands close behind her shoulder, reading the slip too, his eyebrows raised, laughing; Fumiko of the third reference picture, on her other side, claps her gloved hands together once, beaming.

THE COURTYARD, across the middle of the picture:
- a large bronze incense burner on a stone base in the middle of the courtyard, white smoke rising from it;
- a wooden stall with a roof, where a young woman in a white kimono top and a plain red divided skirt hands small paper charms to visitors;
- a few visitors in winter coats wait on the steps; among them a young woman in a bright kimono of red and gold with a white fur stole;
- two large stone guardian lions sit on stone pedestals, one on each side of the steps, with a little snow on their heads.

THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- at the top of the stone steps, the main hall of the shrine, of dark old wood with a vast curved grey tiled roof powdered with snow;
- in front of the hall, a long wooden offering box, and above it a thick rope of plain straw hangs from a large round bronze bell; a man in a black coat pulls the rope;
- on the left, a tall gate of plain vermilion wood at the entrance of the courtyard;
- tall old cedar trees rise behind the hall, dark green with snow on their branches, against a clear pale-blue winter sky.

The shrine is crowded, cold and festive: between the objects and the people, the same bare grey stone, the same bare old wood and the same clean white snow.
