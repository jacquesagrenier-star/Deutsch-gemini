A real photograph of a small old family restaurant in Lisbon at lunchtime on an October weekday, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a diner would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at sitting eye height from a neighbouring table, looking across the young man's table towards the kitchen door and the blackboard, everything in sharp focus. Daylight from the street window and warm light from wall lamps; the textures of real paper tablecloths, glazed earthenware, wood, glass, bread crust, olives, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tablecloths are plain white paper, the plates are plain white earthenware, the blackboard shows only chalk drawings of a fish and a pig, the jug is plain white, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- a small square table with a white paper tablecloth crosses the bottom of the picture;
- on it, the remains of a feast: an empty bread basket with crumbs, an empty dish with only olive stones, an empty little plate where butter was, a jug of red wine and a glass;
- the young man of the reference pictures sits at the table on the left of the middle, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he leans back in his chair, both hands on his full belly, satisfied, his cheeks round.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- on the left, an old couple eat grilled fish at the next table;
- on the right, the waiter, a man of fifty in a white shirt and a black waistcoat, arrives carrying a big plate of grilled meat with rice and chips in one hand, and with the other hand he lays a small paper bill on the table, with a polite and very knowing smile.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- a blackboard on the wall with chalk drawings of a fish and a pig;
- the open kitchen door, with steam and a cook in a white hat at the stove;
- a television high on the wall, its screen dark, and a framed photograph of a football team.

The room is small and lively: between the tables, the same bare tiled floor and the same bare white walls.
