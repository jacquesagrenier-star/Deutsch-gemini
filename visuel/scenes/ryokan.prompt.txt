A real photograph of a guest room in a traditional Japanese inn in the mountains near Kyoto on an October evening, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend at the door of the room would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the entrance of the room, looking across the tatami towards the wide window and the steaming outdoor bath in the garden beyond, everything in sharp focus. Warm lamplight inside and the blue light of dusk outside; the textures of real woven straw, dark polished wood, rice paper, ceramic, lacquer, printed cotton, stone, steam, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here both wear the inn's cotton robe of white with a plain blue pattern of small squares, tied with a plain dark-blue sash; the young woman's curls are damp from the bath.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are plain sand-coloured plaster and dark wood, the sliding doors are plain cream paper, the hanging scroll in the alcove shows only a painted branch of red maple on plain paper, the cushions are plain dark-red cloth, the cups are plain white, and the television screen is dark. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tatami, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE ROOM, across the lower half of the picture:
- the floor is pale-green tatami mats with dark cloth borders;
- in the middle of the room, a low square table of dark lacquered wood; on it, a small black iron teapot, two white tea cups, a small dish with two wrapped sweets, and a wooden room key on a large wooden tag;
- around the table, flat square dark-red cushions on the tatami, each with a low wooden backrest;
- on the right, two futons are already laid out side by side on the tatami, with plain white covers and folded white blankets at their ends;
- Yui of the third reference picture kneels at the table, pouring tea from the iron teapot into one of the cups, looking up at her friend with a teasing smile;
- the young woman of the reference pictures stands in the middle of the room, seen whole from her head to her bare feet on the tatami, facing the camera, proudly showing off her cotton robe with both arms spread a little from her sides, grinning; a small white towel is folded over her shoulder.

THE ALCOVE AND THE WALLS, across the middle of the picture:
- on the left, a shallow alcove with a raised floor of polished wood; in it hangs the scroll with the painted maple branch, and below it a small ceramic vase with one branch of red leaves;
- beside the alcove, on a low wooden stand, a small television with a dark screen and, below it, a small refrigerator of dark wood;
- a round lamp of paper in a wooden frame stands in the corner, lit, glowing warm.

THE WINDOW AND THE GARDEN, across the upper third of the picture:
- the far wall is a wide window in a wooden frame, its paper screens slid fully open; in front of it, a narrow sitting area of polished boards with two low armchairs and a small round table;
- beyond the glass, in the garden lit by stone lanterns, an outdoor bath of dark rocks filled with steaming water, empty, the steam rising into the dusk;
- around the bath, large mossy rocks, a few bamboo plants, and maple trees with bright red leaves;
- behind the garden, the dark shapes of wooded mountains under a deep blue evening sky.

The inn is calm and warm: between the objects and the people, the same bare tatami, the same bare dark wood and the same plain sand-coloured walls.
