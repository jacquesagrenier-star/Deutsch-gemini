A real photograph of the check-in area of the departure hall of Berlin airport on a bright morning, taken as a traveller waiting in the queue a few metres away would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, daylight from a tall glass wall at the back and white ceiling lights. The textures of real polished stone floor, brushed metal, glass, hard plastic, leather, paper, fabric and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a navy-blue wool coat open over a light-grey sweater, blue jeans, white sneakers and a red scarf around her neck.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the screens are dark, the sign hanging from the ceiling shows only a white arrow on a blue board, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, the passport is a plain dark-red booklet, the ticket is a plain white strip of paper, the suitcases carry no tags, the airplane is plain white with no logo, and nothing carries a sign, a brand or a name badge.

THE FRONT of the picture, the lower part:
- on the left, a large grey hard-shell suitcase lying on the black belt scale beside the counter;
- a small red cabin suitcase standing upright on its wheels next to the young woman;
- on the right, a metal luggage trolley with a green backpack on it;
- a bottle of water with no label standing on the floor beside the trolley.

THE COUNTER on the left, a light-grey check-in counter seen from slightly to the side, with a dark computer screen turned towards the clerk, a white telephone, a plain white paper ticket lying on the counter and a small green plant in a pot at its end.

THE CLERK, a woman of about fifty with short grey hair, in a dark-blue uniform jacket over a white blouse with a blue scarf, stands behind the counter and smiles at the young woman while she takes her passport.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands in front of the counter, seen whole from her head to her feet, which stand on the polished floor with their shadow beside them. She is caught in the middle of checking in: she holds out her dark-red passport across the counter to the clerk with one hand, her other hand rests on the handle of the small red suitcase beside her, with a cheerful smile. A brown leather handbag hangs from her shoulder.
