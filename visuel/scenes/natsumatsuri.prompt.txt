A real photograph of a summer festival in the grounds of a neighbourhood shrine in an old part of Tokyo on a warm evening at the end of August, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend in the crowd would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in the lane between the food stalls, looking along the lane towards the shrine at the far end, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from strings of paper lanterns and the bulbs of the stalls under a deep blue evening sky; the textures of real printed cotton, wood, canvas awnings, paper, water, ice, gravel, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a summer cotton kimono of indigo with large white morning-glory flowers, tied with a plain yellow sash, her curls pinned up, and Yui a summer cotton kimono of pale pink with a plain red sash; both wear wooden sandals.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the paper lanterns are plain red and plain white, the awnings of the stalls are plain red-and-white stripes, the paper cups and plates are plain white, the masks are plain white faces with simple painted eyes, and the boards of the stalls are bare wood. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare gravel or bare wood around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the pale gravel of the shrine grounds, lit warm by the stalls;
- on the left, a goldfish stall: a long shallow blue tub of water full of small orange and red goldfish; a small boy in a blue summer kimono crouches over it, holding a little paper scoop above the water, his tongue between his teeth;
- on the right, the young woman of the reference pictures and Yui of the third reference picture walk side by side towards the camera, seen whole from their heads to their wooden sandals on the gravel; the young woman holds a paper cup of shaved ice with bright red syrup and a small spoon, laughing with her mouth full; Yui, beside her, holds a round paper fan and points with it at something further down the lane, smiling.

THE STALLS, across the middle of the picture:
- on both sides of the lane, a row of food stalls under red-and-white striped awnings, each lit by bare bulbs;
- on the left, a stall where a man of about sixty with a white towel tied around his head fries noodles on a big iron griddle, steam and smoke rising; beside it a stall with a wall of white masks hanging in rows;
- on the right, a stall of shaved ice with a block of clear ice and bottles of red, green and yellow syrup, and a woman in a white apron handing a cup to a small girl in a yellow summer kimono;
- people stroll along the lane between the stalls: an old man with a little girl on his shoulders, and a young couple in summer kimonos.

THE BACK OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- at the end of the lane, the shrine: a small wooden hall with a sweeping grey tiled roof, a large drum on a wooden stand in front of it, and a man in a white jacket beating the drum with two sticks;
- a big tree with dark leaves stands beside the hall;
- strings of red and white paper lanterns hang across the whole lane from one stall to the other, all lit, glowing;
- above them, the deep blue evening sky with the first stars and a thin crescent moon.

The festival is joyful and crowded: between the objects and the people, the same bare gravel, the same bare wood of the stalls and the same plain striped awnings.
