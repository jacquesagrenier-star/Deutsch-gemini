A real photograph of a town square in a residential district of Berlin on a sunny early afternoon in late spring, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a passer-by would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 35 mm lens from a first-floor window on one side of the square, looking across and down onto it, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm daylight, soft shadows, and the textures of real stone, brick, copper, water, sand, bark, leaves, fabric and skin.

The first two reference pictures show the young man, the third and fourth the young woman: their faces, hair, build and height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the two people only; here he wears a dark-green jacket over a grey T-shirt and jeans, and she wears a light-blue denim jacket over a white T-shirt and dark jeans.

Every surface in the picture is plain: shop windows show only their goods, awnings are plain cloth, the plinth of the statue is bare stone. Each object stands clearly on its own, with bare paving, grass or wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE NEAR SIDE OF THE SQUARE, along the bottom edge of the picture, from left to right:
- a small playground on sand, quiet for the moment: a red slide, a wooden swing frame with two empty swings, and a square sandpit with a blue plastic spade and bucket left in the sand;
- a large leafy chestnut tree, its trunk standing on the paving;
- a wooden park bench under the tree, with an old man in a flat cap sitting on it and throwing breadcrumbs to a few grey pigeons on the paving in front of the bench;
- a dark-green litter bin on a post at the end of the bench;
- a round flower bed full of red and yellow tulips.

THE MIDDLE OF THE SQUARE, on light-grey paving stones:
- in the centre of the picture, a round stone fountain with a low wide rim and a single jet of water rising from a stone bowl in its middle;
- the young man of the reference pictures sits on the near rim of the fountain, turned three-quarters towards the left of the picture; he has just seen her and lifts the hand nearer to her above his shoulder, his palm open towards her, smiling;
- the young woman of the reference pictures walks towards him from the left of the picture, a few steps away from the fountain, her phone held loosely down at her side in one hand, smiling back at him: she has found her way;
- on the left of the fountain, a young mother pushing a dark-blue pram; the young woman is level with her, and both women have the same height;
- on the right of the fountain, a bronze statue of a man in a long coat on a tall square stone plinth;
- in front of the statue, a street musician sitting on a folding stool, playing an acoustic guitar, its open case on the paving in front of him;
- further right, a man in a light raincoat walking a brown dog on a lead.

THE BUILDINGS around the far side of the square fill the upper part of the picture, under a strip of blue sky, from left to right:
- a red-brick church with a tall pointed green copper spire and a large round stained-glass window above its arched wooden door;
- the town hall in the middle, the largest building, pale sandstone with tall arched windows, a wide stone staircase up to its main door, and a square tower rising above its roof with a small round window near its top;
- a bookshop: a dark-blue shopfront, its window full of books standing upright with plain coloured covers, a small table of books outside its door;
- an ice-cream parlour on the right: a white-and-pink striped awning over a counter window with metal tubs of ice cream in pale colours, and two small round tables with chairs in front of it.
