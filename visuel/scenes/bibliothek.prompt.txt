A real photograph of the reading room of a university library in Berlin on a winter afternoon, taken as a fellow student sitting across the table would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight from tall windows on the left and warm green desk lamps. The textures of real wood, paper, leather, fabric, glass, metal, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a cream knitted sweater and dark jeans.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the book spines are plain coloured, the open pages are blank, the laptop screen shows only a plain blue background, the notes are blank yellow squares, and nothing carries a sign or a logo.

THE TABLE fills the lower half of the picture: a long dark wooden reading table seen from slightly above. On it, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a green glass desk lamp, switched on;
- a pile of three thick books with plain red, blue and green covers;
- a big open book with blank pages;
- an open laptop showing a plain blue screen;
- a pair of white headphones lying on the table;
- an open notebook with blank pages and a yellow pencil lying across it;
- a small pad of yellow sticky notes;
- a bottle of water with no label;
- a pair of glasses folded on a closed book.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures sits at the table on a wooden chair, seen from her head down to the table top, which hides her below the waist. She is caught in the middle of studying: she sits upright, her chin resting on one hand, and only her other hand reaches forward to turn a page of the big open book, with a concentrated little smile. A grey backpack hangs from the back of her chair.

THE ROOM behind her, from the left of the picture to the right:
- tall windows with white frames, snow on the roofs outside;
- a tall green plant in a pot on the floor near the windows;
- long wooden shelves full of books with plain coloured spines, up to the ceiling;
- a wooden library ladder leaning against the shelves;
- a large old globe on a wooden stand;
- at the back, a staircase with a wooden handrail going up to a gallery;
- a round wall clock with a plain white face and no numbers;
- at a second table further back, a young man with dark curly hair reads a magazine.
