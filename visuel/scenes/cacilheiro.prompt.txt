A real photograph of the open upper deck of the orange ferry that crosses the river from Lisbon to Cacilhas on a windy February afternoon, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a passenger would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height at the back of the deck, looking across it towards the city on the far bank, everything in sharp focus. Bright winter sunlight and sparkling water; the textures of real painted steel, wooden benches, ropes, water, bread, wool, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the hostel receptionist, the young man with the black beard: his face, his black beard, his build and his grey wool cardigan stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the ferry is plain orange and white, the benches are plain wood, the life rings are plain orange, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare deck around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle by the rail, seen from his head down to his knees; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; his hands are still raised, open and empty, at the height of his mouth, and his mouth is open wide in shock;
- a big seagull flies off just above his hands with his whole pork sandwich in its beak;
- beside him on the right, the bearded colleague of the third reference picture bites into his own sandwich, laughing so hard he nearly chokes.

THE MIDDLE OF THE DECK, from left to right:
- wooden benches where passengers sit: a woman reading, a boy with a bicycle;
- an orange life ring on the rail and coiled ropes.

THE RIVER, across the upper third of the picture:
- the wide sparkling river and, on the far bank, the city of Lisbon on its hills with the castle on top;
- on the right, the long red suspension bridge;
- other seagulls in a blue sky.

The deck is open and windy: between the people, the same bare painted deck.
