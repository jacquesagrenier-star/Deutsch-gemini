A real photograph of the fish hall of a covered market in Lisbon on a December morning, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a shopper would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in the aisle, looking along the fish stalls, everything in sharp focus. Cool daylight from the high glass roof and white lamps above the stalls; the textures of real crushed ice, fish scales, wet stone, steel, rubber aprons, paper, wool, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the owner of the pastry shop, the man with the thick white moustache: his face, his thick white moustache, his bald head, his build and his red-and-blue checked shirt stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the price signs are plain white cards with only numbers, the crates are plain white plastic, the aprons are plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wet stone floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the fish stall crosses the bottom of the picture: beds of crushed ice with sardines, a big sea bream, mackerel, an octopus and prawns, and a steel scale;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands in front of the stall, on the left of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he takes one startled step back, his hands raised, because the big sea bream on the ice stares at him with its round eye.

THE MIDDLE OF THE HALL, from left to right:
- behind the stall, the fishwife, a strong woman of fifty in a yellow rubber apron and rubber gloves, holds up a big fish by the tail and calls out with her mouth wide open;
- on the right, the owner of the pastry shop of the third reference picture, a basket on his arm, laughs at the young man;
- a woman with a shopping trolley chooses prawns.

THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- rows of other stalls with fruit and vegetables;
- the high iron roof with glass panels and a big round clock hanging from a beam;
- seagulls perched on the iron beams.

The hall is busy but orderly: between the stalls, the same bare wet stone floor.
