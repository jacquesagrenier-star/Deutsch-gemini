A real photograph of the dining room of an old Lisbon apartment on Christmas Eve, during the family dinner, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a guest would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at sitting eye height from the end of the table, looking along it towards the window and the Christmas tree, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from a chandelier and candles on the table, the night blue in the window; the textures of real white linen, china, glass, salt cod, cabbage, a cake with candied fruit, wood, wool, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the landlady, the small round old woman with short white hair: her face, her short white hair, her build and her apron printed with large red flowers stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the tablecloth is plain white linen, the plates are plain white china, the tablet screen shows only the face of an old smiling woman, the paper crowns are plain gold, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tablecloth or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the long table crosses the bottom of the picture: a big dish of boiled salt cod with potatoes, cabbage and eggs, a jug of olive oil, glasses of wine, and in the middle a big ring-shaped cake decorated with candied fruit, one slice cut;
- the young man of the reference pictures sits on the left side of the table, seen from his head down to his waist; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds up between two fingers a small dried broad bean he has just found in his slice of cake, his face torn between pride and worry.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- around the table, the family applauds and laughs: the landlady of the third reference picture at the head of the table, her son and his wife, and two teenage grandchildren;
- on the table near the young man, a tablet leans against the jug, its screen showing the face of an old smiling woman with grey hair waving from far away;
- a sleeping cat on a chair.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- a Christmas tree with plain red and gold balls and lights, and a nativity scene on a small table;
- a window with lace curtains and the night outside;
- the yellow canary in its white cage on top of a cupboard.

The room is warm and crowded but each person sits clearly on their own: between them, the same bare white tablecloth and the same bare walls.
