A real photograph of a fireworks display over a wide river in Tokyo on a summer night at the end of August, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend sitting just behind would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at seated eye height from the grassy riverbank, behind two young women sitting on a sheet, looking up and across the river at the fireworks bursting in the sky, everything in sharp focus. The bright, many-coloured light of the fireworks and its reflection on the water, the city lights beyond; the textures of real grass, water, printed cotton, paper, concrete, steel and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from behind, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a summer cotton kimono of indigo with large white morning-glory flowers, tied with a plain yellow sash, her curls pinned up, and Yui a summer cotton kimono of pale pink with a plain red sash.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the round paper fans are plain white, the sheet is plain bright blue, the boats are plain white with rows of plain red paper lanterns, and the buildings across the river are plain dark shapes with lit windows. Each thing stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare grass, bare water or bare sky around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE RIVERBANK, across the lower third of the picture:
- the dark green grass of the sloping riverbank, with a plain bright-blue sheet spread on it;
- in the middle of the sheet, the young woman of the reference pictures and Yui of the third reference picture sit side by side, seen from behind, their faces turned up towards the sky in three-quarter view, lit by the colours of the fireworks; the young woman holds her old black film camera up towards the sky with both hands, and its flash has just gone off, a burst of white light; Yui leans her head towards her, holding a round paper fan against her chest, laughing;
- beside them on the sheet, two paper cups, a can of plain silver beer, and their wooden sandals set side by side;
- around them, other people sit on the grass in the dark, seen from behind: a family with two small children, one child standing and pointing, a couple sharing a fan; and just in front of the two friends, an old man on a folding stool, his bald head lit up bright white by the flash, has turned his head halfway round, blinking and amused.

THE RIVER, across the middle of the picture:
- the wide dark river, its surface shining with the reflections of the fireworks in red, gold and green;
- two long low wooden pleasure boats float on the water, each with a row of plain red paper lanterns along its roof, lit;
- on the left, a long steel bridge crosses the river, lined with small white lights, and dark figures of people stand along its rail;
- on the far bank, a line of trees and the dark shapes of tall buildings with lit windows.

THE SKY, across the upper half of the picture:
- three great fireworks burst in the black night sky: a huge golden chrysanthemum of falling sparks in the middle, a red ring to the left, and a green and white burst to the right, with trails of smoke drifting between them;
- the sparks fall slowly towards the river.

The night is warm and festive: between the people and the river, the same bare dark grass and the same open dark water.
