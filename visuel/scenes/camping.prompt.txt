A real photograph of a small campsite on the shore of a lake in the forest of Brandenburg on a summer evening, at dusk, taken as a friend sitting on the other side of the campfire would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, the warm orange light of the fire on the faces and the deep blue of the evening sky. The textures of real wood, fabric, nylon, stone, water, grass, metal, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a dark-red fleece jacket, khaki trousers and hiking boots.

Every thing stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing or print, and nothing carries a logo or a brand.

THE FRONT of the picture, the lower part, is short grass and earth:
- a small campfire in a ring of grey stones, with bright flames;
- a black metal pot hanging over the fire from a little tripod;
- a small pile of firewood next to the fire;
- a torch lying on the grass, switched on;
- a red mug standing on a flat stone.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures sits on a fallen tree trunk on the left of the fire, seen whole from her head to her boots, which rest on the grass. She is caught in the middle of playing the guitar: an acoustic guitar on her knee, her fingers on the strings, singing with a happy smile, the firelight on her face.

THE MIDDLE of the picture:
- on the right of the fire, a young man with dark curly hair and a blue hoodie sits on the grass, roasting a sausage on a long stick over the fire;
- behind them, a green dome tent with its door open and a rolled sleeping bag in front of it;
- a blue backpack leaning against the tent;
- a small grey mushroom growing at the foot of a tree.

THE BACK of the picture:
- the calm lake reflecting the sky, with a red wooden rowing boat pulled up on the shore;
- tall pine trees of the forest on both sides;
- the dark blue evening sky with a bright half moon and the first few stars.
