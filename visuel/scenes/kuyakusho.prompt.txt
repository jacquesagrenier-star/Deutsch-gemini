A real photograph of the residents' service floor of a Japanese ward office in Tokyo on a spring weekday morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a visitor waiting nearby would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from just behind and to the left of a customer seated at the counter, looking across the counter into the office beyond it, everything in sharp focus. Even white ceiling light and daylight from tall windows; the textures of real pale vinyl floor, laminated wood, painted steel, paper, hard plastic, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third from behind: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt and the black jeans, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the paper on the counter is a sheet of empty boxes drawn in thin grey lines, the small paper ticket is plain white, the screens are dark, the folders and boxes on the shelves are plain single colours, the walls are plain pale grey, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare counter, bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE COUNTER, across the lower half of the picture:
- the young woman of the reference pictures sits on a low grey stool on the customer side of a long, low, pale-wood counter, on the near side of it, in the lower middle of the picture; we see her from behind and a little from her side: her back, her curly hair, her shoulder in the mustard-yellow jacket, and her face in profile, turned towards the clerk; the counter is in front of her, and nothing stands between her and the camera;
- on the counter in front of her lies the sheet of empty boxes; she holds a black ballpoint pen just above it, caught in the middle of writing, her pen stopped, her cheeks a little pink, a sheepish smile on her face;
- beside the sheet, on the counter: her plain dark-navy-blue passport lying closed, its cover blank, her notebook with a plain red cover lying open on a blank page, and the small plain white ticket;
- across the counter, a clerk in her forties with short black hair and round glasses, in a white blouse and a plain navy cardigan, sits upright on the far side of the counter; only her arm reaches across it, one finger resting on one box of the sheet, showing it, and she smiles kindly at the young woman;
- her olive-green canvas shoulder bag hangs from the back of the stool; her white sneakers rest on the bar of the stool.

THE WAITING AREA, on the left, in the middle of the picture:
- two rows of pale-blue chairs joined on steel bars; an old woman in a grey coat sits on the front row with a cloth bag on her lap; a young mother sits beside her with a small baby asleep on her shoulder; behind them a man in a dark suit and tie reads a folded newspaper;
- at the end of the rows, a grey ticket machine on a stand, a box the height of a person, with one plain white paper ticket sticking out of its slot.

THE OFFICE, across the upper third of the picture, behind the clerk:
- rows of grey steel desks pushed together, each with a computer whose dark screen faces the person sitting at it; three office workers in white shirts sit working at them, seen from the side;
- against the back wall, tall grey steel shelves with plain blue folders and plain white cardboard boxes in tidy rows;
- on the back wall, a round wall clock above the shelves;
- on the right, a tall window with its light-grey blinds half raised, the sky pale and bright beyond it, and a tall green plant in a white pot standing beside it;
- the ceiling is white panels with long rows of white lights.

The office is orderly and quiet: between the objects and the people, the same bare vinyl floor, the same bare pale-wood counter and the same plain pale-grey walls.
