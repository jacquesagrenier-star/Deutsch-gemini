A real photograph of the aisle of a small neighbourhood supermarket in Berlin on a weekday afternoon, taken as a friend standing in the aisle a few metres away would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, bright white ceiling lights and a little daylight from the shop window at the back. The textures of real metal, glass, plastic, cardboard, paper, bread crust, fruit skin, fabric and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a dark-green rain jacket open over a grey sweater, blue jeans and white sneakers.

Every product stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the packets, bottles and cans have plain coloured surfaces with no labels and no logos, the price tags on the shelves are plain blank white strips, and the shop has no signs.

THE FRONT of the picture, the lower part:
- a red plastic shopping basket standing on the floor tiles, with a loaf of bread, a carton of eggs and a bunch of bananas with plain unmarked yellow skins in it;
- a metal shopping trolley on the right, seen from the side, with a large paper bag and a bottle of water with no label in it.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands in the aisle between the shelves, seen whole from her head to her feet, which stand on the floor tiles with their shadow beside them. She is caught in the middle of choosing: she holds a glass bottle of milk with no label in one hand and a small round cheese in the other, and looks from one to the other with an amused, hesitant smile. A black purse hangs from her shoulder.

THE SHELVES on the left of the aisle, from top to bottom:
- a row of glass bottles of juice, orange and red, with no labels;
- a row of plain cans in red and green, with no labels;
- packets of pasta and bags of rice in plain paper packets with no print;
- a few bars of chocolate in plain brown wrappers.

THE SHELVES on the right of the aisle, a glass-door fridge:
- pots of yoghurt in plain white pots;
- blocks of butter in plain gold paper;
- sausages lying in a tray.

THE BACK of the picture:
- at the end of the aisle, the checkout with a black conveyor belt;
- a cashier, a man of about sixty with a grey beard and glasses, in a dark-blue shop jacket, sits at the checkout;
- next to the checkout, a little girl of about five in a yellow raincoat holds her father's hand and points at the shelf of chocolate;
- the shop window at the back, with the grey street and a parked bicycle outside.
