A real photograph of the tatami living room of an old wooden family house in Kanazawa late on New Year's Eve, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a relative standing in the doorway would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the sliding door, looking down a little at a family round a heated low table, towards the family altar and the snowy window, everything in sharp focus. Warm lamplight and the soft glow of the television; the textures of real woven straw, quilted cotton, dark polished wood, lacquer, ceramic, steam, knitted wool, rice paper and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her grandmother Fumiko, a Japanese woman of eighty-two, the third shows her father Hiroshi, a Japanese man of fifty-eight: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, Fumiko's face, short white hair and build, and Hiroshi's face, short greying hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain mustard-yellow knitted sweater, Fumiko a plain grey cardigan, and Hiroshi a plain dark-brown knitted sweater.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the quilt is plain dark-blue cloth, the bowls are plain black lacquer with plain red insides, the cups are plain white, the photograph on the family altar shows a smiling old man against a plain grey background, the television screen shows only a soft glow of blue and gold light, the walls are plain sand-coloured plaster, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare table, bare tatami or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE TABLE, across the lower half of the picture:
- a low square heated table on the tatami, its top on a thick plain dark-blue quilt that hangs to the floor; everyone's legs are under it;
- on the table, five black lacquer bowls of hot buckwheat noodles in broth, each topped with a large fried prawn and a few slices of green spring onion, steam rising; pairs of chopsticks; a small white bottle of sake with tiny cups; a teapot and cups; and a basket of mandarin oranges;
- at the far side, side by side, Fumiko of the second reference picture and Hiroshi of the third reference picture: Fumiko lifts noodles with her chopsticks and looks up at her son with a happy smile; Hiroshi, his bowl in his hands, looks back at his mother, his eyes moist and smiling;
- on the left, the young woman of the reference pictures, seen from her side in three-quarter view, holds her bowl close and slurps a long noodle, her cheeks full, watching her father and grandmother with delight;
- on the right, the aunt Keiko, a Japanese woman of about fifty-five with short black hair and round tortoiseshell glasses, in a plain lilac sweater, pours sake into a tiny cup; beside her, the boy Daichi, eleven, with short black hair, in a plain blue sweater, has fallen asleep with his head on his folded arms on the table.

THE ROOM, across the middle of the picture:
- against the left wall, the tall family altar of dark lacquered wood, open, with the framed photograph of the smiling old man, a small bowl of rice, a vase of pine branches, and in front of it a New Year offering of two round white rice cakes stacked one on the other with a small orange on top;
- on the right, a low cabinet with a television on it, its screen glowing softly;
- a round wall clock with a black rim above the cabinet.

THE WINDOW, across the upper third of the picture:
- the far wall has a window of paper screens in a wooden lattice, one screen slid open; through the glass behind it, the dark night, a garden thick with snow, and snowflakes falling in the light from the room;
- from the dark wooden ceiling hangs a round paper lamp, lit, glowing warm.

The room is warm, quiet and full of family: between the objects and the people, the same bare tatami, the same bare dark wood and the same plain sand-coloured walls.
