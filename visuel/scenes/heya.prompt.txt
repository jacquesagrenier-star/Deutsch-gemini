A real photograph of a small, old-fashioned Japanese room of six tatami mats on the upper floor of an old wooden house in Tokyo, on a sunny spring morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend at the door would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens from the open sliding door at standing eye height, looking into the room and slightly down, towards the window at the far end, everything in sharp focus. Soft morning sunlight from the window; the textures of real woven straw, bare wood, rice paper, cotton cloth, corduroy, hard plastic, steel, ceramic and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Her clothes here are the white t-shirt and the black jeans of the reference pictures, with plain white socks; her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket lies folded on the lid of her suitcase.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are plain cream plaster, the sliding doors of the cupboard are plain cream paper in a thin wooden frame, the clothes and the bedding are plain coloured cloth, the box is plain brown cardboard, the notebook has a plain red cover, the photograph in its frame shows two smiling faces against a pale sky, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare tatami, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the pale-green tatami mats with their dark cloth borders fill the bottom of the picture;
- on the right, her red hard-shell suitcase lies open flat on the tatami, its lid folded back with the mustard-yellow jacket on it; in the open case, neat piles of folded clothes: a grey knitted sweater, two white shirts and a few rolled pairs of socks;
- on the left of the suitcase, the young woman of the reference pictures sits on the tatami with her legs folded to one side, seen whole, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the right of the picture; she is caught in the middle of lifting a folded blue shirt out of the suitcase with both hands, looking down at it with a small happy smile;
- at the bottom left corner, her olive-green canvas shoulder bag lies on the tatami, and a clear plastic umbrella leans against the wall beside the door frame.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM:
- on the left wall, a wide built-in cupboard with its paper sliding door pushed open; on its upper shelf, a folded white futon mattress with a folded pale-blue blanket on it; on its lower shelf, one plain brown cardboard box;
- in the middle of the room, a low square wooden table standing on the tatami; on it, an old black film camera, the notebook with the plain red cover with a black pen lying on it, a white mug, and a small round alarm clock;
- along the right wall, a small kitchen corner: a short steel counter with a small steel sink, a two-ring gas cooker with a small steel pot on it, and under the counter a small white refrigerator; on the wall above the counter, a small round mirror.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- the far wall has a wide window in a wooden frame, its glass panes open to the morning, with plain white curtains drawn to either side;
- on the low wooden windowsill, a small framed photograph and a small green plant in a white pot;
- through the window, the grey tiled roofs of the neighbouring houses, a tall green tree, and a pale-blue morning sky;
- from the ceiling of the room hangs a round white lamp.

The room is small, bright and almost empty, as on a first morning: between the objects and the person, the same bare tatami, the same bare wood and the same plain cream walls.
