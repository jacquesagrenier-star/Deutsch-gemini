A real photograph of the open-air flea market of Lisbon on a sunny April Saturday morning, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a shopper would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in an alley between the stalls, everything in sharp focus. Bright spring sunlight and the shade of trees; the textures of real old tiles, worn leather, brass, wood, cloth spread on the ground, paper, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the classmate in the green cap: his face, his green cap and his build stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the old tiles are plain blue on white with simple flowers, the old books have plain worn covers, the cloths on the ground are plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare ground around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- a cloth spread on the ground with old things laid out: tiles, keys, an old radio, brass candlesticks, records and a pair of boots;
- the young man of the reference pictures crouches on the left, seen whole; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds up proudly in both hands an old blue and white tile panel showing a painted yellow canary in a cage, beaming;
- his classmate of the third reference picture stands beside him and haggles with the seller, three fingers raised.

THE MIDDLE OF THE MARKET, from left to right:
- the seller, a big man with a cigarette behind his ear, sitting on a folding chair under a parasol;
- other stalls with old clothes on hangers, a mirror and an old bicycle.

THE BACK, across the upper third of the picture:
- a large white church with a dome above the market;
- trees with fresh green leaves;
- a blue spring sky.

The market is lively but each thing lies clearly on its own: between the stalls, the same bare dusty ground.
