A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the inside of a small neighbourhood hair salon in Berlin on a Saturday morning, taken as a customer sitting on the waiting sofa a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, soft daylight from the shop window on the right and warm light from a lamp. The textures of real wood, steel, glass, ceramic, leather, cotton, wool, paper, hair and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Here her hair is loose and falls to her shoulders. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a light-blue blouse, dark jeans and brown ankle boots.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the bottles on the shelf are plain white and brown bottles with no labels, the magazines show only a plain coloured cover with no title, the cash register has a dark display, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, the picture on the wall is a plain painting of flowers, and nothing in the salon carries a sign, a logo, a brand or a price.

THE MIDDLE of the picture: a black leather salon chair on a round steel foot, standing in front of a large mirror on the left wall. The mirror is seen at a sharp angle from the side, so that it shows only the pale wall opposite.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures sits in the chair, seen whole from her head to her ankle boots, which rest on the steel footrest of the chair. A plain white towel lies around her shoulders over her blouse. She holds a closed magazine on her lap with both hands and smiles at her reflection, her head held still.

THE HAIRDRESSER, a man of about fifty with short grey hair and round glasses, in a black shirt and grey trousers, stands behind her chair and is caught in the middle of the cut: he holds a lock of her hair between two fingers of one hand and a pair of steel scissors open in the other hand, just above the lock. A few cut blond hairs lie on the floor around the foot of the chair.
