A real photograph of a steep shopping street of Lisbon paved with black and white stones on a rainy January afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a passer-by would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height in the middle of the sloping street, looking up it, everything in sharp focus. Grey rainy daylight and the warm glow of shop windows on the wet stones; the textures of real wet black and white paving stones, umbrellas, raincoats, glass shop windows, cotton, rubber and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the waitress, the young woman with round red glasses: her face, her dark hair pinned up, her round red glasses and her build stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the umbrellas are plain single colours, the shop windows show plain goods without signs, the raincoats are plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wet paving around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the wet pavement of small black and white stones, shining with puddles;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; his black umbrella has been turned inside out by the wind, its ribs sticking up, and he holds it up looking at it in dismay, his hair dripping;
- on the right, a small old lady in a plastic rain hat and shiny red rubber boots walks up the slope past him, perfectly calm, pulling a shopping trolley.

THE MIDDLE OF THE STREET, from left to right:
- in a shop doorway on the left, the waitress of the third reference picture, in a yellow raincoat, laughs and holds out her own umbrella towards him;
- people pass under umbrellas of red, green and blue;
- a lit shoe shop window and a lit bookshop window.

THE TOP OF THE STREET, across the upper third of the picture:
- the street climbs between old pastel facades with iron balconies;
- a yellow tram waits at the top of the slope;
- a heavy grey sky and slanting rain.

The street is wet and grey but each person stands clearly on their own: between them, the same bare shining paving.
