A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the living room of an old Berlin flat on moving day, a Saturday afternoon, taken as a person standing in the doorway a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, bright daylight from a tall window at the back, white walls and a wooden floor. The textures of real cardboard, wood, paper, fabric, glass, metal, plants and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a red checked flannel shirt with rolled-up sleeves over a white T-shirt, blue jeans and white sneakers.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the cardboard boxes are plain brown with no print and no writing on them, the roll of tape is plain brown, the books have plain coloured spines with no titles, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, and nothing carries a sign, a logo or a brand.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands in the middle of the room, seen whole from her head to her sneakers, which stand on the wooden floor with their shadow beside them. She is caught in the very moment of carrying a big plain cardboard box in both arms, with a green plant in a pot sticking out of the top of the box, and she smiles over the box at her younger brother. A bunch of keys hangs from the belt loop of her jeans.
