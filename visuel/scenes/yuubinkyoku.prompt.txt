A real photograph of the inside of a small neighbourhood post office in Tokyo, which is also a savings bank, on a June morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer waiting on the bench would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from beside the waiting bench, looking towards the parcel counter, everything in sharp focus. Even white ceiling light and daylight from the glass front; the textures of real pale vinyl floor, laminated wood, painted steel, brown paper, cardboard, packing tape, glass, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build, her height and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the parcel is wrapped in plain brown paper with a blank white label, the envelopes and postcards are blank, the stamps are small plain coloured squares, the screens of the cash machines are dark, the scale shows a dark display, the walls are plain pale cream, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare counter, bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- on the left, a low padded bench of plain grey, on which an old man in a beige jacket sits waiting, holding a white envelope on his knee;
- on the right, a tall standing desk of pale wood with a few blank postcards, a pen on a string and a stack of plain white envelopes on it.

THE COUNTER, across the middle of the picture:
- a long counter of pale wood runs across the picture, with a clear glass screen standing on it;
- in front of the counter, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the clerk on the right of the picture; she pushes a square parcel in brown paper across the counter with both hands, her chin lifted, proud of her parcel; her sneakers stand on the vinyl floor with their soft shadow beside them; her olive-green canvas bag hangs at her hip, and her open brown wallet lies on the counter beside the parcel;
- behind the counter, the clerk, a young man in a white shirt and a dark-blue vest, holds a blank white label in his hand, pointing with a pen at the blank address lines on the label, smiling helpfully;
- on the counter between them, a flat grey parcel scale with a dark display, a small tray of coins, and a sheet of small coloured stamps;
- beside the counter, a small queue: a woman in a grey suit holding a white envelope, and a schoolboy in a white shirt holding a small box.

THE BACK OF THE OFFICE, across the upper third of the picture:
- on the left, a row of two cash machines of the savings bank, steel boxes with dark screens, side by side against the wall;
- on the right, behind the counter, two desks with grey office chairs, a computer with a dark screen and a tall grey shelf of plain blue folders;
- just inside the glass front door at the far left, a tall red post box with a rounded top;
- a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands on the back wall, and a tall green plant in a white pot in the corner;
- the ceiling is white panels with long white lights.

The post office is small and orderly: between the objects and the people, the same bare vinyl floor, the same bare pale-wood counter and the same plain cream walls.
