A real photograph of a cherry-blossom picnic in a park in Tokyo on a sunny Sunday in early April, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend standing at the edge of the picnic would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height, looking down a little at a group sitting on a blue groundsheet, and beyond them along a path lined with cherry trees towards a pond, everything in sharp focus. Bright spring sunlight filtered through the pale-pink blossoms; the textures of real woven plastic sheet, grass, gravel, bark, petals, water, paper, cotton, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt and the black jeans, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the groundsheet is plain bright blue, the lunch boxes are plain black with clear lids, the cans are plain silver, the bottles of tea are plain green, the cups are plain white paper, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare groundsheet, bare grass or bare gravel around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE PICNIC, across the lower half of the picture:
- a large plain bright-blue groundsheet is spread on the grass under the trees; along its near edge, at the bottom of the picture, five pairs of shoes stand neatly side by side on the grass, their toes pointing away from the sheet: three pairs of black office shoes, one pair of beige flat shoes, and the young woman's pair of white sneakers;
- in the middle of the groundsheet, a few things are laid out: three plain black lunch boxes with clear lids showing white rice, fried chicken and vegetables, a stack of white paper plates, pairs of wooden chopsticks, a small packet of sweets, two plain green bottles of tea, and a few plain silver cans of beer;
- four office workers sit round the food in their weekday clothes, shoes off, in white shirts and dark trousers or a dark skirt, their jackets folded beside them: on the left, a cheerful man of about fifty with his tie loosened sits upright and cross-legged, his arms reaching out towards the young woman, holding a silver can in both hands and pouring beer into her cup; beside him a young man and a young woman laughing; at the back of the sheet, a woman in her thirties holding up her paper cup;
- the young woman of the reference pictures sits on the right of the groundsheet with her legs folded to one side, in her socks, seen whole, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the man on the left; she holds her white paper cup out towards him in one hand, while her other hand is raised in a little wave of thanks, laughing; her olive-green canvas bag lies beside her, and an old black film camera hangs on a brown leather strap around her neck.

THE PATH, across the middle of the picture, beyond the picnic:
- a wide gravel path runs from the left edge of the picture into the distance, lined on both sides by cherry trees in full bloom, their dark trunks and branches holding clouds of pale-pink blossom; a few petals lie on the gravel;
- on the path, an old man in a beige jacket walks a small white dog on a red lead; two small children in yellow caps run along the path ahead of their mother; a woman pushes a bicycle;
- on the right of the path, a wooden bench under a tree, empty, with a few pigeons pecking on the gravel in front of it.

THE BACK OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- the path leads to a wide calm pond, and over it a small arched wooden bridge painted red;
- beyond the pond, more cherry trees in bloom and a few tall green trees;
- overhead, the blossoming branches of the nearest cherry tree reach across the top of the picture against a clear blue sky with one or two small white clouds.

The park is festive and peaceful: between the objects and the people, the same bare blue groundsheet, the same bare grass and the same bare gravel path.
