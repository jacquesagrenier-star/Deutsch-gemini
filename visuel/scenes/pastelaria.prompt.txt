A real photograph of the inside of a small neighbourhood pastry shop in Lisbon on a sunny September morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the end of the counter, looking along it towards the coffee machine and the back wall, everything in sharp focus. Morning daylight from the shop window on the left and warm light from round lamps above the counter; the textures of real marble, glass, chrome, glazed tiles, white china, pastry, paper, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the wall tiles are plain glazed squares in blue and white, the cups and plates are plain white china, the paper napkins are plain white, the bottles are plain glass, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare marble or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the long glass display case of the counter starts at the bottom right corner of the picture and runs away from the camera on a diagonal towards the back of the shop; behind its glass, in rows on white trays, golden custard tarts with dark caramelised tops, round cakes dusted with sugar, and crusty bread rolls;
- on the left, on the customers' side of the shop, the young man of the reference pictures stands seen whole from his head down to his knees; the counter is beside him, on the right of the picture, his elbow resting on its white marble top; between him and the camera there is only the open tiled floor of the shop; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds a tiny white espresso cup on its saucer very close to his face and stares into it, his eyebrows raised, lost and puzzled, as if the cup were much smaller than the coffee he ordered;
- on the white marble counter beside his elbow, a sugar bowl, a small spoon, a paper napkin and a plate with one custard tart.

THE MIDDLE OF THE SHOP, from left to right:
- on the left, through the large shop window, the sunny cobbled street and the yellow side of a passing tram;
- in the centre, behind the counter, the waitress, a young woman of twenty-five with dark hair pinned up with a yellow pencil stuck in it and round red glasses, in a white blouse and a black apron, holds a tall glass of milky coffee on a saucer; she presses her lips together and looks at the young man sideways, trying hard to keep from laughing;
- on the right, behind the counter, the owner, a man of sixty-four with a thick white moustache and a red-and-blue checked shirt, works the big chrome coffee machine, a white cup under its spout;
- at the far right end of the counter, an old man in a flat cap sits on a high stool reading a folded newspaper, a glass of orange juice beside him.

THE BACK WALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- the wall is covered in plain blue and white glazed tiles;
- shelves of dark wood hold rows of glass bottles and a stack of white cups and saucers;
- a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands;
- a small old till of cream-coloured metal at the end of the counter, under the shelves.

The shop is small and lively but uncluttered: between the objects and the people, the same bare marble counter and the same bare tiled wall.
