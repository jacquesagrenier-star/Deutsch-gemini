A real photograph of the great avenue of Lisbon on the 25th of April, during the parade for the Carnation Revolution, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as someone in the crowd would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height on the pavement, looking along the avenue, everything in sharp focus. Bright spring sunshine through the trees; the textures of real red carnations, cloth banners, stone pavement, leaves, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the waitress, the young woman with round red glasses: her face, her dark hair pinned up, her round red glasses and her build stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the banners are plain red cloth, the flags are the plain green and red Portuguese flag, the balloons are plain red, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare pavement around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle, in his sweatshirt, seen from his head down to his knees; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he holds a single red carnation in both hands, moved, with a gentle smile;
- beside him, the waitress of the third reference picture holds the arm of her grandmother, a tiny old lady of ninety in a black coat, who has just given him the carnation and pats his hand.

THE MIDDLE OF THE AVENUE, from left to right:
- the parade walks down the avenue: people of all ages carrying red carnations, a man with a carnation in his buttonhole, children on their parents' shoulders;
- plain red banners and green and red flags.

THE AVENUE, across the upper third of the picture:
- the wide avenue lined with tall plane trees in fresh spring leaves;
- old buildings with balconies where people lean out;
- a clear blue sky.

The avenue is full but each person in front stands clearly on their own, with bare pavement around their feet.
