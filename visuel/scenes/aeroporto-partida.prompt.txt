A real photograph of the same baggage hall of Lisbon airport as in the third reference picture, at the end of August, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a traveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from beside the baggage belt, looking across the hall towards the exit doors, everything in sharp focus. Bright late-summer daylight from the windows; the textures of real polished stone floor, rubber, steel, glass, a birdcage, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

The third reference picture shows the place: the same baggage hall of Lisbon airport: the polished stone floor, the baggage belt, the grey seats, the clock, the tall windows and the exit doors stay exactly as in it.

The fourth reference picture shows the landlady, the small round old woman with short white hair: her face, her short white hair, her build and her apron printed with large red flowers stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens are dark, the suitcases are plain single colours, the clothes are plain cloth, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare polished floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:

- the newly arrived traveller, a young man with a big backpack and a plain red tuque, looks lost and nods.

THE MIDDLE OF THE HALL, from left to right:
