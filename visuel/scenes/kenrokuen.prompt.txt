A real photograph of a famous old Japanese landscape garden in Kanazawa covered with fresh snow on a calm January morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a visitor on the path would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the edge of a pond, looking past a stone lantern towards a small stone bridge where a father and his daughter stand, everything in sharp focus. Soft white winter light under a pale grey sky; the textures of real snow, ice, still water, granite, pine needles, straw rope, bark, wool coats and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her father Hiroshi, a Japanese man of fifty-eight: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Hiroshi's face, short greying hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain dark-green wool coat, a long plain mustard-yellow knitted scarf, plain brown gloves and plain brown boots, and Hiroshi a plain dark-grey wool coat, a plain grey knitted hat and plain brown leather gloves.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the stone lantern is plain weathered granite, the umbrella is plain dark red, the ropes are plain straw, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare snow, bare water or bare stone around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE POND, across the lower half of the picture:
- the snowy bank of the pond along the bottom edge, with a few round stones capped with snow;
- the still dark water of the pond, with thin ice at its edges and the reflection of the trees in it;
- on the left, standing in the water on two legs of stone, a tall granite lantern with a wide round roof thick with snow, one of its two legs longer than the other;
- two white and grey birds stand at the edge of the ice.

THE BRIDGE, across the middle of the picture:
- a small arched bridge of grey stone slabs crosses a narrow arm of the pond, its rails covered with a line of snow;
- on the bridge, the young woman of the reference pictures and Hiroshi of the third reference picture stand side by side, seen whole from their heads to their boots and shoes on the snowy stone; the young woman holds her old black film camera up to her eye with both hands, photographing her father, laughing behind the camera; Hiroshi stands close beside her, pointing with one gloved hand at the great pine tree on the right and explaining with a warm smile, while a clump of snow that has just fallen from an overhanging pine branch sits on top of his head and on his shoulders, white on his dark hair;
- beyond the bridge, a snowy path with two visitors under a plain dark-red umbrella, seen from behind.

THE TREES, across the upper third of the picture:
- on the right, a great old pine tree with long twisting branches, protected by dozens of straw ropes that run in a tall cone from the top of a wooden pole at its centre down to each branch, all the branches heavy with snow;
- more pine trees and bare cherry trees in the background, white with snow;
- behind them, the dark tiled roof of an old wooden tea house with snow on it;
- above, a pale grey winter sky, with a few snowflakes still falling.

The garden is silent, white and peaceful: between the people and the trees, the same bare snow, the same still water and the same grey stone.
