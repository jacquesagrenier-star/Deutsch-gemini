A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the inside of a small bicycle repair shop in a Berlin neighbourhood on a bright afternoon, taken as a customer standing by the door a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, daylight from the shop window on the right and a warm ceiling lamp. The textures of real steel, rubber, worn wood, concrete, oil, cardboard, fabric and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.
