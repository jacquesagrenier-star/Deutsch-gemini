A real photograph of a mountain path through a forest of red maple trees in Japan on a clear November afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a hiker a few steps behind would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height on the path, looking towards a small wooden bridge over a mountain stream and the red and gold slopes beyond, everything in sharp focus. Clear autumn sunlight through the leaves; the textures of real earth, fallen leaves, mossy rocks, rushing water, weathered wood, bark, knitted wool, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket over a plain grey knitted sweater, black jeans and plain brown walking boots, and Yui a plain dark-green quilted jacket, plain beige trousers, white sneakers and a plain cream knitted hat.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the bridge is bare weathered wood, the bench is bare wood, the backpack is plain red, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare earth, bare water or bare rock around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the earth path, covered with fallen red, orange and yellow maple leaves;
- on the left, a large grey rock with green moss on it, and a few small stones beside the path;
- on the right, a plain wooden bench at the edge of the path, with a few leaves on its seat.

THE BRIDGE, across the middle of the picture:
- a small arched wooden footbridge with low wooden rails crosses a clear mountain stream that tumbles between round grey rocks, white water foaming around them;
- on the bridge, the young woman of the reference pictures and Yui of the third reference picture walk side by side towards the camera, seen whole from their heads to their shoes on the planks of the bridge; the young woman holds her old black film camera hanging on its strap in one hand and points with the other hand up at the trees, her face lifted, amazed; a single red maple leaf sits on top of her curly hair like a little hat; Yui, beside her, holds up a second red maple leaf by its stem next to it, comparing the two, laughing; Yui carries a small plain red backpack;
- behind them on the path, an old man with a walking stick and a cap rests on a rock, looking at the stream;
- a small brown bird sits on a rock in the stream.

THE SLOPES, across the upper third of the picture:
- beyond the bridge, the steep mountainside is covered with maple trees in full autumn colour, bright red, orange and gold, with a few dark-green cedars between them;
- on the left, a narrow white waterfall falls down a dark rock face into the stream;
- higher up, half hidden among the red trees, the curved grey tiled roof of a small mountain temple;
- above the ridge, a strip of clear blue sky with one small white cloud.

The forest is quiet and glowing: between the people and the trees, the same bare earth path, the same bare grey rocks and the same clear running water.
