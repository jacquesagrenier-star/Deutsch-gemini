A real photograph of the checkout of a small neighbourhood supermarket in an old part of Tokyo on a May evening, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer waiting in the queue would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the end of an aisle, looking towards the till, with the fresh-food counters along the side, everything in sharp focus. Bright, even white ceiling light; the textures of real glossy floor tiles, steel shelving, clear plastic film, crushed ice, paper, cardboard, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build, her height and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: each packet, bottle, carton and tray is a single solid colour with a plain band of a second colour, the price cards are blank white cards, the screen of the till is dark, the small card in the cashier's hand is plain pale blue, and the shopping bags are plain white. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare shelf, bare counter or bare floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- on the left, a stack of red plastic shopping baskets standing on the floor;
- on the right, a low wooden stand of fresh vegetables and fruit in shallow green crates: round green cabbages, bunches of long white radishes, red tomatoes with plain unmarked skins, a crate of red apples with plain unmarked skins and a crate of small oranges with plain unmarked skins, each crate with a blank white price card;
- between them, the pale glossy floor tiles.

THE TILL, across the middle of the picture:
- a white checkout counter with a black till whose screen is dark; on the counter, the young woman's red plastic basket with a few things in it: a carton of eggs, a carton of milk, a loaf of white bread in clear film, a pack of tofu and a green bottle of tea;
- behind the counter stands the cashier, a cheerful woman of about sixty in a plain green apron over a white blouse, holding up a small plain pale-blue card towards the young woman with one hand, the other hand resting on the counter, with a questioning smile;
- in front of the counter, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the right of the picture where the cashier is; she holds her open brown wallet in one hand and a few coins in the other, looking at the pale-blue card with her head tilted, puzzled, half laughing; her sneakers stand on the floor tiles with their soft shadow beside them; an olive-green canvas shoulder bag hangs at her hip;
- behind her, an old man in a grey cap waits his turn with a basket holding a fish wrapped in clear film and a bottle of soy sauce.

THE BACK OF THE STORE, across the upper third of the picture:
- along the left wall, a long open refrigerated counter of fresh fish on crushed ice, whole silver fish and pink fillets on white trays, and next to it trays of red meat and pale chicken in clear film;
- along the back wall, tall white shelves with bags of rice, bottles of soy sauce, packets of sugar and salt, all in plain colours;
- a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands on the back wall;
- the ceiling is white with long rows of bright fluorescent tubes.

The store is tidy and busy in a quiet way: between the objects and the people, the same bare glossy floor, the same bare white shelving and the same plain white walls.
