A real photograph of the rooftop terrace of the youth hostel in the Alfama district of Lisbon on an August evening, during a farewell party, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a friend would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height on the terrace, looking across it towards the river, everything in sharp focus. The golden light of the setting sun and strings of small bulbs; the textures of real terracotta tiles, wooden crates, plastic chairs, glasses, cake, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build and his height stay exactly as in them; it is summer, and he wears his white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, his dark-blue jeans and his white canvas sneakers.

The third reference picture shows the hostel receptionist, the young man with the black beard: his face, his black beard, his build and his grey wool cardigan stay exactly as in it.

The fourth reference picture shows the waitress, the young woman with round red glasses: her face, her dark hair pinned up, her round red glasses and her build stay exactly as in it.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the sweatshirt is plain red, the cake is plain white, the bunting is plain colours, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare terracotta floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands in the middle, seen from his head down to his knees, facing the camera; laughing, he lets the bearded colleague of the third reference picture pull a plain red hooded sweatshirt over his head, on top of his royal-blue one;
- the waitress of the fourth reference picture holds a cake with one candle and laughs.

THE MIDDLE OF THE TERRACE, from left to right:
- a young man in a green cap claps his hands;
- a table of wooden crates with glasses, bottles and a bowl of crisps;
- plastic chairs and a guitar leaning against the wall.

THE BACK, across the upper third of the picture:
- the terracotta roofs of Alfama tumbling down towards the river;
- the river shining gold, and a big white ship;
- an orange evening sky.

The terrace is joyful and open: between the people, the same bare terracotta floor and the whole city beyond.
