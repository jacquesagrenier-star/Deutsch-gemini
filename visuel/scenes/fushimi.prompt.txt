A real photograph of a mountain path lined with hundreds of vermilion shrine gates in Kyoto on a clear October morning, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a friend a few steps behind would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height inside the tunnel of gates, looking up the stone steps towards the light at the far end, everything in sharp focus. Soft morning sunlight filtering through the gaps between the gates and the trees; the textures of real painted wood, black lacquer, mossy stone, gravel, autumn leaves, corduroy, cotton, leather and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them; she also wears a plain grey knitted sweater under the jacket.

Every surface in the picture is plain: each gate is two round pillars and two crossbeams of plain bright vermilion with plain black bases, the stone foxes are plain grey stone, the stone lanterns are plain weathered stone, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare stone, bare gravel or bare wood around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the path is wide stone steps, worn smooth, with a little gravel and a few red and yellow leaves on them;
- at the bottom left, beside the first gate, a stone fox sits on a stone pedestal, upright, with a red cloth bib tied around its neck and a stone key in its mouth;
- at the bottom right, a squat stone lantern with a little moss on its roof.

THE PATH, across the middle of the picture:
- the vermilion gates stand one behind another in a long tunnel, so close that their pillars almost touch, climbing the steps and curving gently to the left in the distance;
- at the bottom left, the young woman of the reference pictures crouches face to face with the stone fox, seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which stand on a stone step with their shadow beside them, in three-quarter view; her old black film camera rests lowered in her lap; her face is level with the fox's face and a hand's breadth away from it, both seen in profile and facing each other, nose to nose, and she has tilted her head to exactly the same angle as the fox, with the same solemn face; her olive-green canvas bag hangs at her hip;
- a few steps above her, two other visitors have stopped on the path to smile at the young woman and the fox: an old couple in beige jackets, the man with a walking stick, the woman with a small cloth bag;
- further up, a young woman in a plain red kimono walks slowly, seen from behind.

THE TOP OF THE PICTURE:
- the gates go on climbing into the distance, smaller and smaller, towards a bright patch of daylight at the end of the tunnel;
- above the tunnel, between the beams, the branches of tall trees: dark-green cedars and a maple with its first red leaves, and small patches of pale-blue sky.

The path is quiet and luminous: between the gates and the people, the same bare grey stone steps, the same bare gravel and the same plain vermilion wood.
