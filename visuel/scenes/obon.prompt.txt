A real photograph of a small hillside cemetery above Kanazawa in mid-August at the end of the afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a relative standing a few steps behind would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height, looking past a family grave towards the roofs of the town and the mountains, everything in sharp focus. Low golden evening sunlight and soft shadows; the textures of real granite, moss, gravel, wood, paper lanterns, wet stone, smoke, flowers, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her grandmother Fumiko, a Japanese woman of eighty-two: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Fumiko's face, short white hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain dark-navy cotton dress, and Fumiko a plain black summer jacket over a plain grey dress.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the faces of the grave stones are smooth polished grey granite, the paper lanterns are plain white, the wooden bucket is bare wood, and the clothes are plain dark cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare gravel or bare stone around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- a path of pale gravel and flat stepping stones runs from the bottom edge up to the family grave;
- on the path, a wooden bucket of water with a wooden ladle in it, and beside it a folded white towel;
- on the left, the boy of eleven in a plain blue baseball shirt crouches, pouring water from a ladle onto the base of the grave, watching it run.

THE GRAVE, across the middle of the picture:
- the family grave, a tall polished grey granite stone standing on two wider steps of stone, with a low stone border around its plot of gravel;
- in front of the stone, two stone vases filled with bright summer flowers, yellow, purple and white; between them a small stone stand where a bundle of incense sticks smokes, a thin line of white smoke rising; on the lower step, an offering of two peaches, a small bunch of grapes and a little cup of sake;
- the young woman of the reference pictures crouches in front of the grave, seen from her side in three-quarter view, her hands pressed together in front of her face, her eyes closed, her lips moving a little as if she were speaking quietly to someone;
- standing just behind her, Fumiko of the third reference picture rests one hand gently on her granddaughter's shoulder, looking at the grave with a calm smile;
- a little to the right, the boy's mother Keiko, a Japanese woman of about fifty-five with short black hair and round tortoiseshell glasses, in a plain black dress, holds a white paper lantern on a short bamboo stick, its candle lit inside, glowing.

THE BACK OF THE PICTURE, across the upper third:
- behind the grave, other grey stones in rows climb the hillside, some with flowers; a flight of stone steps goes up between them;
- a row of white paper lanterns hangs on a line between two wooden posts at the edge of the cemetery, already lit;
- beyond, below the hill, the tiled roofs of the town, the curved roof of an old temple among green trees, and far away the blue mountains;
- the sky is warm gold and pale blue, with a few long thin clouds.

The cemetery is peaceful and tidy: between the objects and the people, the same bare gravel, the same bare grey stone and the same quiet green hillside.
