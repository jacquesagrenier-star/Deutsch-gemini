A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the inside of a small neighbourhood bakery in Berlin early on a weekday morning, taken as a customer standing by the door a few metres away would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm light from the ceiling lamps and grey daylight from the shop window on the right. The textures of real bread crust, flour, glass, wood, paper, ceramic, metal, fabric and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a dark-green wool coat over a cream sweater, blue jeans and brown ankle boots.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: there are no price tags on the shelves, the cash register has a dark screen, the paper bags are plain brown, the cake boxes are plain white, the newspaper shows only grey illegible lines, the wall clock has a plain face and no numbers, and nothing carries a sign, a logo or a brand.

THE COUNTER runs across the middle of the picture, a wooden counter with a glass front, seen from slightly to the side. On the counter top, from the left of the picture to the right:
- a black cash register with a dark screen;
- a small metal scale;
- a wooden board with a long bread knife on it;
- a plain white cake box, closed;
- a basket of bread rolls;
- a tray of pretzels.
Inside the glass front of the counter: a round strawberry cream cake, a slice of apple cake with soft baked apple pieces, unmarked skins and no stickers, on a small plate, and a plain nut cake.

THE SELLER, a woman of about fifty with dark hair under a white cap, in a white blouse, stands behind the counter and holds out a brown paper bag of rolls across the counter to the young woman, smiling.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands in front of the counter, seen whole from her head to her feet, which stand on the tiled floor with their shadow beside them. She is caught in the middle of paying: she takes the brown paper bag with one hand and holds out a single coin to the seller with the other, smiling back. A brown leather handbag hangs from her shoulder.

THE SHELVES on the wall behind the counter, from top to bottom:
- a row of round and long loaves of bread;
- a row of glass bottles of juice, orange and red, with no labels, and a glass bottle of milk with no label;
- a plain paper bag of flour and a glass jar of sugar;
- a white coffee pot and a few white cups.
Above the shelves, a round wall clock with a plain white face and no numbers, and at the back on the left, the steel door of a big oven.

THE RIGHT SIDE of the picture, by the shop window:
- a small round wooden table with two wooden chairs;
- at the table, an old man of about seventy-five with white hair and round glasses, in a grey cardigan, sits with a cup of coffee and a slice of cake on a plate in front of him, reading a folded newspaper; his wooden walking stick leans against the table;
- a small brown dog lying on the floor under the table;
- a few yellow flowers in a drinking glass on the table;
- the shop window with a white curtain pulled aside, and outside the grey street with a bicycle leaning against a lamp post;
- a green plant in a pot on the floor beside the window.
