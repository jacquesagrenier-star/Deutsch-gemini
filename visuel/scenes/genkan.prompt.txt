A real photograph of the entrance hall of an ordinary Japanese family house and the living room beyond it, on a quiet afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a guest would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens from the open front door at standing eye height, looking straight down the short corridor into the living room, everything in sharp focus. Soft afternoon daylight coming through paper screens, warm wood everywhere; the textures of real ceramic tiles, polished wood, woven straw, rice paper, cotton cloth, leather, fur and skin.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the walls are plain cream plaster, the wooden surfaces are bare wood, the picture on the wall shows only a painted mountain on plain white paper, the cushions and the quilt are plain coloured cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE ENTRANCE, along the bottom of the picture:
- the lower floor of the entrance, dark grey ceramic tiles, fills the bottom edge of the picture; on it stand three pairs of shoes neatly side by side with their toes towards the door: a pair of brown men's leather shoes, a pair of white sneakers, and a small pair of red children's shoes;
- at the left edge, a tall white umbrella stand holding one closed black umbrella and one closed red umbrella;
- at the right, a low wooden shoe cabinet against the wall, its doors closed, and on its top a small blue ceramic vase with three white flowers and a shallow wooden dish with a bunch of keys in it;
- above the cabinet, a round wooden wall hook with a long camel coat hanging from it and a grey knitted hat hanging from the next hook;
- a single wooden step, the height of a knee, rises from the tiles to the polished wooden floor of the corridor; a young man in a dark-blue sweater and jeans sits sideways on this step, seen in profile from his left side, one foot still in its black shoe on the tiles and the other foot, in a grey sock, resting on the wooden floor, his hands untying the lace of the shoe; his black shoes are the fourth pair;
- on the wooden floor just above the step, a pair of pale-blue house slippers set side by side, ready to be stepped into.

THE CORRIDOR, across the middle of the picture: a short corridor of polished pale wood, with plain cream walls; on the left wall, a round white wall clock with a black rim; on the right wall, a framed picture of a painted mountain on plain white paper; at the end of the corridor, a wide sliding door with a wooden lattice and white paper panes stands open, slid fully to the left, so that the living room is seen through it.

THE LIVING ROOM, across the upper part of the picture, seen through the open sliding door:
- the floor is pale-green straw tatami mats with their dark cloth borders;
- in the middle of the room, a low square wooden table whose top sits on a thick plain dark-red quilt that hangs down on all four sides to the tatami; on the table top, a small round brown clay teapot, two white tea cups and a bowl of mandarin oranges;
- around the table, four flat square plain blue cushions on the tatami, one on each side; a woman in her fifties in a beige cardigan kneels on the far cushion, seen from the front, her hands pouring tea from the teapot into one of the cups, looking down at the cup; a tabby cat lies curled up asleep on the cushion at the left;
- against the left wall of the room, a low wooden television cabinet with a flat black television on it, its screen dark;
- against the far wall, a window of white paper screens in a wooden lattice, glowing with soft daylight, and under it a tall green potted plant in a brown pot;
- from the ceiling of the room hangs a round white paper lamp, lit, glowing warm.

The house is tidy and lived-in: between the objects and the people, the same bare tiles, the same bare polished wood, the same bare tatami and the same plain cream walls.
