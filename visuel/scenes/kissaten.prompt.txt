A real photograph of a small old-fashioned coffee house in an old part of Tokyo on a busy July afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer at a table would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at seated eye height from a table near the window, looking towards the counter where two young women are working, everything in sharp focus. Bright summer daylight through lace curtains and warm lamps; the textures of real dark polished wood, worn leather, brass, white porcelain, glass, ice, lace, paper, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her colleague Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here both wear the same work clothes, a plain white shirt with the sleeves rolled up and a plain dark-green apron, with black trousers.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the cups are plain white porcelain, the glasses are clear, the trays are plain dark wood, the newspaper and the sheets of paper are plain grey and white, the screen of the till is dark, the walls are bare dark wood and plain cream plaster, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE TABLES, along the bottom of the picture:
- in the foreground, the edge of a small wooden table with a white cup of black coffee, a glass of iced coffee with ice cubes and a straw, and a slice of strawberry cake on a white plate with a small fork;
- at the next table on the left, an old man in a grey summer shirt has lowered his folded newspaper and watches the young woman over the top of it with an amused smile, his cup of tea in front of him;
- at a table on the right, a young mother and her small daughter in a yellow dress share a glass of orange juice and a slice of cake.

THE COUNTER, across the middle of the picture:
- a long dark wooden counter with a black till with a dark screen at its left end;
- behind the counter, the young woman of the reference pictures, in her apron, carries a dark wooden tray with two white cups of coffee and a glass of milk towards the end of the counter, holding it with both hands and making a polite little bow over it as she walks, her eyes fixed on the cups, the coffee trembling right up to their rims, a concentrated smile on her face;
- beside her, Yui of the third reference picture stands close, her mouth near her friend's ear, whispering something, one hand raised beside her mouth, her eyes twinkling;
- on the counter: a gleaming steel coffee machine, a row of clean white cups upside down on a white cloth, a glass jar of sugar cubes, a glass dome over a round cake, and a small vase with one white flower.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- on the back wall, wooden shelves with rows of white cups, glass jars of coffee beans and plain-coloured books;
- a round white wall clock with a black rim and two black hands, and a painted landscape of green hills in a wooden frame;
- on the right, the glass door of the coffee house with a small brass bell above it, and the sunny street outside;
- two lamps with cream glass shades hang from the ceiling, lit, and a ceiling fan turns slowly between them.

The coffee house is warm and lively: between the objects and the people, the same bare dark wood, the same bare counter and the same plain cream walls.
