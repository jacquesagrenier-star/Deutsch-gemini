A real photograph of a small room in a music school in Berlin on a weekday evening, taken as a classmate sitting near the door would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, warm light from a floor lamp and the last daylight from a window at the back. The textures of real wood, varnish, metal, strings, paper, fabric, leaves and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a dark-green T-shirt and blue jeans.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing or print: the sheet music shows only plain lines and notes with no words, the posters are plain coloured pictures of instruments, and nothing carries a logo or a brand.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures sits on a wooden chair in the middle of the room, seen whole from his head to his sneakers, which stand on the wooden floor with their shadow beside them. He is caught in the middle of his guitar lesson: an acoustic guitar on his knee, his left hand on the neck, his right hand on the strings, his eyes on the sheet music, concentrated, with the tip of his tongue between his lips.

THE TEACHER, a woman of about sixty with short white hair, round glasses and a long purple scarf, sits on a chair facing him on the right, her own guitar on her knee, showing him a chord with her fingers and smiling.
