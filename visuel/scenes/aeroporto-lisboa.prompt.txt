A real photograph of the baggage hall of Lisbon airport on a bright September afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a traveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from beside the baggage belt, looking across the hall towards the exit doors and the tall windows beyond, everything in sharp focus. Bright daylight from the windows and soft white ceiling lights; the textures of real polished stone floor, rubber, brushed steel, glass, hard plastic, paper, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens are dark, the board hanging from the ceiling is a plain blue rectangle, the suitcases and bags are plain single colours, the airplane is plain white with one plain green stripe, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the right of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers, which stand on the polished pale-grey stone floor with their soft shadow beside them; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture, his back half turned to the baggage belt; he holds a mobile phone to his ear and smiles warmly as he talks, his eyes on the floor, his free hand open in a little explaining gesture;
- on the left of the picture, the long low baggage belt crosses the foreground on a slight diagonal, dark grey rubber plates edged with brushed steel; on it, just behind the young man's back, his bright grass-green hard-shell suitcase rides past him all alone, the only suitcase left on the belt;
- a little girl of six in a yellow cap stands at the end of the belt beside her mother's legs, pointing at the green suitcase with her whole arm, her mouth open in delight.

THE MIDDLE OF THE HALL, from left to right:
- on the left, an empty steel luggage trolley stands by the belt;
- in the centre, a woman in a red coat pushes a trolley loaded with two black suitcases towards the exit, a folded newspaper on top of them;
- on the right, a row of three grey seats joined on a steel bar; an old man in a grey jacket sits on the end seat with a brown travelling bag on his knees, watching the green suitcase go by with raised eyebrows;
- on the white wall behind the seats, a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands.

THE BACK OF THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- on the left, the wide sliding glass doors of the arrivals exit stand open, and through them a man waits holding a bunch of yellow flowers;
- across the back, tall windows in large clear panes held by thin steel frames;
- through the glass, on the grey airfield, a plain white airplane stands seen from the side, with one plain green stripe along its body, and beyond it a row of cream-coloured taxis with green roofs;
- above the airfield, a deep-blue September sky with one soft white cloud.

The ceiling is white panels with rows of round lights, and the plain blue board hangs from it on thin steel rods. The hall is calm and spacious: between the objects and the people, the same bare polished stone floor and the same bare white walls.
