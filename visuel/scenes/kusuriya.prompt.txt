A real photograph of the inside of a small neighbourhood pharmacy in Tokyo on a cold late-November afternoon, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer near the door would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the entrance, looking along the shelves towards the counter at the back, everything in sharp focus. Bright, even white ceiling light; the textures of real glossy floor tiles, white steel shelving, cardboard boxes, glass, paper, knitted wool, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her friend Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears her mustard-yellow corduroy jacket over a plain grey knitted sweater, a long plain mustard-yellow knitted scarf wrapped twice around her neck, and black jeans; Yui wears a plain dark-green wool coat.

Every surface in the picture is plain: each box and bottle on the shelves is a single solid colour with a plain band of a second colour, the price cards are blank white cards, the screen of the till is dark, the paper bag is plain white, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare shelf, bare counter or bare floor around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- on the left, a stack of red plastic shopping baskets;
- on the right, a low white bench for waiting customers, with an old man in a brown coat sitting on it, a paper bag on his knees;
- the pale glossy floor tiles between them.

THE SHELVES AND THE COUNTER, across the middle of the picture:
- along the left wall, tall white shelves of small boxes of medicine in plain pale colours, bottles of syrup in plain brown glass, boxes of white paper masks, packets of tissues and rolls of bandage;
- along the right wall, shelves of soaps, toothbrushes, towels and bottles of shampoo in plain colours, and a small round mirror on a stand;
- at the back, the white counter of the pharmacy, with a black till with a dark screen, a small tray, and a plain white paper bag folded at the top;
- behind the counter stands the pharmacist, a woman of about forty with her hair tied back and thin metal glasses, in a white coat, holding a small white box of medicine in one hand and showing it to the young woman, explaining kindly, her lips pressed together on a smile, the other hand resting on the counter;
- in front of the counter, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, which stand on the floor tiles with their shadow beside them, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the pharmacist on the right of the picture; she has a white paper mask pulled down under her chin, red cheeks and tired eyes, and she acts out her illness for the pharmacist like a game of charades: one hand on her forehead, the other on her stomach with a crumpled tissue in it, her eyes rolled up and her mouth pulled into a big dramatic grimace;
- beside her, Yui of the third reference picture stands close, one hand on her friend's back, looking at the pharmacist and nodding, trying hard to keep a straight face.

THE BACK OF THE SHOP, across the upper third of the picture:
- behind the counter, a wall of small white drawers in neat rows, and a tall shelf of white boxes;
- a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands above the counter;
- the ceiling is white with long rows of bright white lights.

The pharmacy is clean, bright and quiet: between the objects and the people, the same bare glossy floor, the same bare white shelving and the same plain white walls.
