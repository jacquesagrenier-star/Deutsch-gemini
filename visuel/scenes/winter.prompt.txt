A real photograph of a quiet snowy street in an old Berlin neighbourhood on a bright winter morning after a night of snow, taken as a neighbour standing on the pavement would take it with a good phone camera, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, low winter sunlight, a clear pale-blue sky. The textures of real snow, ice, wool, wood, brick, metal, glass and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman in three-quarter view, the second from the front: her face, her dark blonde hair, her build and her height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; her clothes here are a long dark-green winter coat, a thick red woollen scarf, a grey knitted hat, brown leather gloves, dark trousers and brown snow boots.

Every thing in the street stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing: no signs, no house numbers, no shop names, no licence plates, no labels.

THE FRONT of the picture, the lower half, is the snowy pavement and a small front garden:
- fresh white snow covers everything, with footprints on the pavement;
- in the garden, a snowman: three balls of snow, two black coal eyes, a carrot for a nose, a few black buttons, an old black hat and a striped scarf;
- a wooden snow shovel stuck upright in the snow next to the snowman, and a broom leaning against the garden wall;
- a small brown dog in a red dog coat sniffing the snow, its paws in the snow.

THE YOUNG WOMAN of the reference pictures stands next to the snowman, seen whole from her head to her boots, which stand in the snow with their shadow on it. She is caught in the middle of finishing the snowman: she places a last black button on its belly with one gloved hand, and smiles at it with a wide smile that reaches her eyes, her breath visible as a small white cloud in the cold air.

THE MIDDLE of the picture:
- a low brick garden wall with snow on top, and a green wooden park bench covered with snow;
- a black iron street lamp with snow on its top;
- a bicycle leaning against the lamp post, its saddle and handlebars covered with snow;
- a small red car parked at the kerb, its roof and bonnet covered with thick snow.

THE BACK of the picture:
- an old four-storey house with a yellow front, white window frames, a dark-green front door with a small wreath of fir branches on it, and snow on the roof;
- in one window, a lit candle on the windowsill;
- on the roof, a chimney with a thin line of white smoke rising into the sky;
- two tall bare trees with snow on their branches, and a small bird sitting on one branch;
- above, the clear pale-blue winter sky.
