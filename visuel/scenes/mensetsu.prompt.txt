A real photograph of a small old-fashioned coffee house in an old part of Tokyo on a late June morning before opening time, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as someone at the counter would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from beside the counter, looking across the room at a table by the window where a job interview is taking place, everything in sharp focus. Soft morning daylight through lace curtains and warm lamps; the textures of real dark polished wood, worn leather, brass, white porcelain, glass, lace, paper, corduroy, cotton and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows the café owner, Mr Sakamoto, a Japanese man of sixty-three, the fourth shows his employee Yui, a Japanese woman of twenty-seven: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair, build and clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt and the black jeans, Mr Sakamoto's face, cropped grey hair and build, and Yui's face, short straight black bob and build, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the cups are plain white porcelain, the sheet of paper on the table is blank white, the walls are bare dark wood and plain cream plaster, the picture on the wall is a painted landscape of green hills on plain paper, the books on the shelf have plain single-colour spines, and the clock has a plain white face with two black hands. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the end of the dark wooden counter, with a glass dome over a round cake on a white plate, a small brass bell, and a sugar bowl with a silver spoon in it;
- the dark polished wooden floor between the counter and the tables.

THE TABLE BY THE WINDOW, across the middle of the picture:
- a small square wooden table with two leather armchairs facing each other;
- on the left chair sits Mr Sakamoto of the third reference picture, in a plain white shirt with the sleeves rolled up, a white cloth over one shoulder, sitting upright, holding the blank sheet of paper in one hand and a white coffee cup in the other, his eyebrows raised in surprise and the corner of his mouth starting to smile;
- on the right chair sits the young woman of the reference pictures, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards him; she leans back comfortably in the armchair with one arm thrown over its back and the other hand lifted in a cheerful, easy wave of explanation, grinning, as relaxed as with an old friend; her white sneakers rest on the floor with their shadow beside them;
- on the table between them, two white coffee cups on saucers, a small white jug of milk and her notebook with a plain red cover;
- standing just behind Mr Sakamoto, Yui of the fourth reference picture, in a white shirt and a plain dark-green apron, holds a small round tray with a coffee pot against her chest; she looks at the young woman with wide eyes, one hand raised to her mouth, holding back a laugh.

THE BACK OF THE ROOM, across the upper third of the picture:
- the window behind the table, with white lace curtains drawn to the sides, the quiet street and a green tree beyond it, and a small vase of white flowers on the sill;
- on the right wall, a tall wooden shelf with rows of white cups and plain-coloured books, and a gleaming steel coffee machine on the counter in front of it;
- on the left wall, the painted landscape in a wooden frame and a round wall clock;
- in the corner, an old upright piano of dark wood with its lid closed, and a tall green plant beside it;
- two lamps with cream glass shades hang from the ceiling, lit.

The coffee house is quiet and warm before opening: between the objects and the people, the same bare dark wood, the same bare floor and the same plain cream walls.
