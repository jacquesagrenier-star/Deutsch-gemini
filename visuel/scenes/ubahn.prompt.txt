A real photograph, taken with a good phone camera and looking exactly like a phone photograph, of the platform of an old underground station in Berlin on a weekday morning, taken as a passenger standing on the platform a few metres away would take it, in a tall vertical picture, everything in sharp focus. Natural colours, white light from the lamps of the station and the warm light from inside the train. The textures of real glazed tiles, granite, steel, glass, painted metal, wood, paper, fabric and skin, with the true depth and the slight lens perspective of a photograph.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second in three-quarter view: his face, his short hair, his build and his height stay exactly as in them. Use them for the person only; his clothes here are a navy-blue padded jacket over a grey hoodie, a dark-green scarf, dark jeans and white sneakers, with a black backpack on his back.

Every object stands on its own, with a little space around it, so that the eye can tell at once where each thing is. Nothing in the picture carries any writing, numbers or print: the train is plain yellow with no number, no line and no words, the screen hanging over the platform is dark, the station wall has no station name, the clock has a plain white face and no numbers, the newspapers at the kiosk show only grey illegible lines, the ticket in his hand is a plain small white card, and nothing carries a sign, a poster, a logo or a brand.

THE RIGHT SIDE of the picture: a yellow underground train stands at the platform with its doors open, seen at a slant from the platform, running from the front of the picture to the back. Through its windows the warm light and a few passengers sitting inside can be seen.

THE YOUNG MAN of the reference pictures stands on the platform in front of the open door nearest to the camera, seen whole from his head to his sneakers, which stand on the grey granite floor with their shadow beside them. He is caught in the very moment of stepping into the train: one foot already lifted towards the door, a small plain white ticket in one hand and a paper cup of coffee in the other, and he looks back over his shoulder towards the camera with a quick smile.

THE LEFT SIDE of the picture, along the tiled wall of the station, from the front to the back:
- a green metal waste bin;
- a wooden bench where an old woman of about seventy with short grey hair, in a beige raincoat, sits with a brown suitcase standing beside her and a red umbrella leaning against the bench;
- a small kiosk with a wooden counter, newspapers and magazines with no titles in a rack, a few bottles of water and a basket of pretzels on the counter;
- a street musician, a man of about forty with a black hat and a grey beard, sits on a folding stool and plays an acoustic guitar, with his open guitar case on the floor in front of him and a few coins in it;
- a staircase going up to the street, with a metal handrail.

THE BACK of the picture:
- the long platform running to the dark tunnel at the end;
- a round clock with a plain white face and no numbers hanging from the ceiling;
- a dark screen hanging from the ceiling over the platform;
- a row of white lamps on the ceiling;
- the pale green glazed tiles of the station walls;
- a small dog on a lead sitting beside the old woman.
