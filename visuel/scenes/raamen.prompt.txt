A real photograph of the inside of a small, narrow noodle restaurant in an old part of Tokyo at lunchtime in May, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a customer at the door would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from just inside the entrance, looking along the wooden counter towards the open kitchen at the back, everything in sharp focus. Warm light from paper lamps and steam rising from the kitchen; the textures of real worn wood, brushed steel, ceramic, glazed tiles, steam, cotton cloth, paper, corduroy and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side: her face, her curly dark-brown hair, her build, her height and her clothes, the mustard-yellow corduroy jacket, the white t-shirt, the black jeans and the white sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the buttons of the ticket machine are plain pale-yellow squares, the small paper tickets are plain white, the bowls are plain black and plain white, the curtain over the kitchen door is plain indigo cloth, the walls are bare wood and plain cream tiles, and the cook's towel is plain white. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare counter, bare wood or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE ENTRANCE, along the bottom of the picture:
- on the left, against the wall, a tall steel ticket machine the height of a person, its front a grid of plain pale-yellow square buttons in neat rows, a coin slot and a note slot on its right side, and a small tray at the bottom holding one plain white paper ticket;
- in front of the machine, the young woman of the reference pictures stands seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, in three-quarter view, her body turned towards the machine on the left of the picture; she holds her small notebook with a plain red cover open in one hand and points at one yellow button with the forefinger of the other hand, her lips pressed together in concentration; her sneakers stand on the dark wooden floor with their soft shadow beside them; her olive-green canvas bag hangs at her hip;
- on the right, a clear plastic umbrella stands in a steel umbrella stand by the door, and beside it an office worker of about thirty in a white shirt and a dark tie waits his turn, standing upright behind her, and only his arm reaches past her shoulder to point at the right yellow button, with a friendly, patient grin.

THE COUNTER, across the middle of the picture:
- a long wooden counter runs from the right edge of the picture towards the kitchen at the back, with a row of six round red stools in front of it;
- two customers sit at the counter, seen from the side: a young man in a dark suit eating noodles from a large black bowl with wooden chopsticks, and an older woman in a beige cardigan lifting a spoon of soup from her white bowl; on the counter in front of them, glasses of water, a jug of water, a box of wooden chopsticks and small bottles of soy sauce;
- in each bowl, noodles in soup with slices of pork, half a boiled egg and green spring onions.

THE KITCHEN, across the upper third of the picture:
- behind the counter, the open kitchen with plain cream tiles; a cook, a man of about fifty with a plain white towel tied around his head and a plain black t-shirt, lifts noodles out of a big steel pot with a wire basket, steam rising around him;
- two more big steel pots stand on the gas burners beside him; on a steel shelf above them, stacks of white and black bowls;
- at the back, an indigo cloth curtain hangs in the kitchen doorway; a small window high on the wall lets in daylight;
- round paper lamps hang above the counter, lit, glowing warm.

The restaurant is small, warm and busy: between the objects and the people, the same bare dark wood, the same bare counter and the same plain cream tiles.
