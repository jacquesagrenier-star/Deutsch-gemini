A real photograph of the steaming volcanic ground of Furnas on the island of São Miguel in the Azores on an August morning, where pots of stew are cooked underground, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a visitor would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height at the edge of the cooking holes, looking across them towards the green hills, everything in sharp focus. Soft morning light through drifting steam; the textures of real steaming earth, iron pots, ropes, wood, green grass, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build and his height stay exactly as in them; it is summer, and he wears only his white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, light-grey shorts and his white canvas sneakers, and his royal-blue sweatshirt is tied around his waist by its sleeves.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the pots are plain dark iron, the cloth bags are plain white, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare earth or bare grass around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- steaming holes in the earth covered by little mounds, each marked with a wooden stake;
- two men in work clothes pull a big iron pot out of the ground with ropes, in a cloud of steam;
- the young man of the reference pictures stands on the left of the middle, seen whole; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he listens with a gentle, moved smile;
- beside him, a small old woman of seventy-nine in a black cardigan, leaning on a walking stick, holds his arm and talks to him warmly.

THE MIDDLE OF THE PICTURE, from left to right:
- a wooden table with plates and a basket of bread waiting for the stew;
- a few other families waiting for their pots.

THE BACK, across the upper third of the picture:
- green hills with hydrangea hedges of blue flowers;
- a calm crater lake below the hills;
- a pale sky with soft clouds.

The place is calm and green: between the people, the same bare earth and the same drifting steam.
