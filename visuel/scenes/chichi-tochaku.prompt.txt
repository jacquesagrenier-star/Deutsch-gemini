A real photograph of the arrivals hall of a Japanese international airport on a winter evening at the end of December, in a tall vertical picture, caught as it happens, as a traveller would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the side of the hall, looking towards the wide arrivals doors where a father has just come out and his daughter runs to meet him, everything in sharp focus. Bright white ceiling lights, and the dark winter night beyond the glass wall; the textures of real polished stone floor, brushed steel, glass, worn leather, wool, knitted cloth, flowers, paper and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young woman from the front, the second shows her from her side, the third shows her father Hiroshi, a Japanese man of fifty-eight: the young woman's face, curly dark-brown hair and build, and Hiroshi's face, short greying hair and build, stay exactly as in them. Use them for the people only; here the young woman wears a plain dark-green wool coat, open, a long plain mustard-yellow knitted scarf, black jeans and white sneakers, and Hiroshi a plain dark-grey wool coat, a plain grey knitted hat, plain brown leather gloves and dark trousers.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens are dark, the boards hanging from the ceiling are plain blue rectangles, the suitcase is old plain brown leather with two leather straps, the flowers are wrapped in plain white paper, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the polished pale-grey stone floor of the hall, reflecting the lights;
- on the left, a row of four grey seats joined on a steel bar, with a woman in a beige coat sitting and waiting, a small black bag on her knees.

THE MEETING, across the middle of the picture:
- in the middle of the picture, Hiroshi of the third reference picture has just stopped walking, seen whole from his head to his shoes on the polished floor; he has let go of the handle of his old brown leather suitcase, which stands upright beside him, and opens his arms wide for a big hug, his gloved hands spread, his face breaking into a broad laugh of surprise;
- the young woman of the reference pictures, coming from the right of the picture, stands just in front of him, seen whole from her head to her white sneakers, and makes him a perfect, formal Japanese bow from the waist, holding out a small bunch of yellow flowers wrapped in white paper in both hands; her face, in three-quarter view, has a mischievous smile, with tears of joy in her eyes;
- around them, a few other arrivals pass with their suitcases: a young couple in ski jackets with a luggage trolley, a businessman in a black coat pulling a small black case, a grandmother being hugged by a little girl in a red knitted hat.

THE BACK OF THE HALL, across the upper third of the picture:
- the wide frosted glass doors of the arrivals exit, standing open, with the bright corridor behind them;
- beside them, a large round white clock with a black rim and two black hands on the white wall;
- on the right, the tall glass wall of the hall, and through it the dark winter night, the lights of the airfield and the white body of an airplane lit by floodlights;
- the ceiling is white panels with rows of round lights, and two plain blue boards hang from it on thin steel rods.

The hall is bright and full of reunions: between the objects and the people, the same bare polished stone floor and the same bare white walls.
