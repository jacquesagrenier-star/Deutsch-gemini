A real photograph of a Portuguese tax office open to the public on a September morning, in a tall vertical picture made of one single continuous view, caught as it happens, as a visitor would take it with a good phone camera. Taken with a 24 mm lens at standing eye height from the entrance of the waiting room, looking across the rows of seats towards the counters, everything in sharp focus. Even white light from the ceiling panels and soft daylight from a high window; the textures of real plastic, painted metal, laminate, paper, glass, cotton, denim and skin.

The first reference picture shows the young man from the front, the second shows him from his side: his face, his wavy dark-brown hair, his short stubble, his build, his height and his clothes, the royal-blue hooded sweatshirt with its red band across the chest, the white t-shirt, the cream scarf with small blue embroidered flowers, the dark-blue jeans and the white canvas sneakers, stay exactly as in them.

Every surface in the picture is plain: the screens are dark, the display panel above the counters is plain black, the forms are plain white paper with grey lines, the chairs are plain grey plastic, and the clothes are plain cloth. Each object stands clearly on its own, with a band of bare floor or bare wall around it, so that the eye can tell at once where everything is.

The picture is built in three bands, from the bottom edge to the top edge.

THE FOREGROUND, along the bottom of the picture:
- the young man of the reference pictures sits on a chair of the first row, on the left of the middle, seen whole from his head to his white sneakers; his body is turned in three-quarter view towards the right of the picture; he has fallen fast asleep after the long wait, his head resting on the shoulder of the old man sitting next to him, his mouth a little open, a small paper ticket still between his fingers;
- the old man beside him, in a grey jacket and a flat cap, sits very straight and wide awake, and looks down at the sleeping head on his shoulder with patient amusement;
- on the chair beside him, his orange notebook and a pen;
- a ticket machine of grey metal stands on a post at the left edge of the picture.

THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM, from left to right:
- rows of grey plastic chairs, where a young woman rocks a baby in a pram, and a man in a suit taps his foot;
- on the right, a water fountain with a stack of paper cups.

THE COUNTERS, across the upper third of the picture:
- three counters behind glass partitions; at the middle one, a clerk with grey hair and glasses stamps a form for a woman in a red coat;
- above the counters, the plain black display panel;
- on the wall, a round white clock with a black rim and two black hands, and a tall green plant in a pot in the corner.

The waiting room is calm and orderly: between the people, the same bare floor and the same bare white walls.
