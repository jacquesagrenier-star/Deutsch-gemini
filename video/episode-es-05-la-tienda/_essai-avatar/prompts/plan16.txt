A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the hips up. The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image.

Delighted: at last a jacket that fits, and he is warm.

He exclaims one word, then says two short sentences. He speaks towards a point just beyond the right edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays hanging at his side, open and empty, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his other hand moves: on « ¡Perfecta! » it takes hold of the open front edge of the mustard-yellow jacket at the height of his chest and draws it a few centimetres forward, showing it off, and it stays holding it.

Across his chest we see only his new plain mustard-yellow padded jacket, open over his plain white shirt, from the first frame to the last.

On the right of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the open wooden floor of the shop and the warm shelves of folded sweaters far behind run on, open and still, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and keeps a wide pleased smile that reaches his eyes, his mouth closed. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the little shop stays warm and soft and out of focus: the rails of clothes, the wooden shelves of folded sweaters, the morning light from the street window. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the right edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
