A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the hips up. The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image.

By now he likes this young newcomer, and it is plain on his face: approval, dry and warm at once. He has watched him set the bottle down beside the bin instead of in it.

He says two words of approval, explains in one short sentence, then states the verdict, deadpan. He speaks towards a point just beyond the right edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays closed round the neck of his brown bottle, resting low in his lap, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his other hand moves: on « Richtig so » it rises, palm down, to the height of his chest and gives one slow pat on the air, approval, then stays there, palm down and calm.

Across his chest we see only his plain bright yellow jacket, zipped to the collar, from the first frame to the last.

On the right of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the lit shop door and the pavement run on, open and quiet, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and keeps a slow, approving half-smile, his mouth closed, his eyes steady on him. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the evening street stays quiet, soft and out of focus, the warm light from the shop window steady on the pavement. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the right edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
