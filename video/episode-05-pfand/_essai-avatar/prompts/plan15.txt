A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the hips up. The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image.

Pure delight: the slip in his hand says real money, and a week ago all of this was rubbish to him.

He reads the amount aloud, then says two words with a laugh. He speaks towards the round dark opening of the machine at the left edge of the picture. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays closed round the small white slip at the height of his chest, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his other hand moves: on « Müll » it comes up and lays itself flat on his chest, over his heart, the delight, and it stays there.

Across his chest we see only his plain dark burgundy sweater and his hand resting flat on it, from the first frame to the last.

On the left of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the pale housing of the machine with its round dark opening stays exactly as it is in the image, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and keeps a wide delighted smile that reaches his eyes, his mouth closed. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the pale wall of the shop entrance and the stack of blue plastic crates stay soft and out of focus. His eyes rest on the round dark opening of the machine at the left edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
