A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the hips up. The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image.

He is proud of this new job and a little nervous about it, and both show.

He answers in two short sentences. He speaks towards a point just beyond the left edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

Only one of his hands is in the picture, the one nearer the right edge, hanging low at his side; the other stays hidden behind the blurred beige shoulder for the whole shot. Only that visible hand moves: on his first word it rises to his chest and lays its fingertips flat on his black T-shirt, just below the collar of his jacket, as if to say « me », and it stays there.

Across his chest we see only his grey jacket, his black T-shirt, plain and unmarked, and his fingertips resting on it, from the first frame to the last.

On the left of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the blurred beige shoulder in the foreground stays exactly as it is in the image, just as blurred, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and keeps a half-smile that reaches his eyes and listens. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the arrivals hall goes on, warm and soft and out of focus, its travellers small and far away, each continuing on their way. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
