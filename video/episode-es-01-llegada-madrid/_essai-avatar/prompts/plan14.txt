He is reassured and hopeful again.

He asks one short question. He speaks towards a point just beyond the left edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays low at his side, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his hand nearer the right edge of the picture moves: on his first words it rises to waist height and points loosely with two fingers past the right edge of the picture, a little downwards, the way ahead, and it stays there while he speaks.

Across his chest we see only his grey jacket and his black T-shirt, plain and unmarked, from the first frame to the last.

On the left of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the blurred beige shoulder in the foreground stays exactly as it is in the image, just as blurred, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and listens without speaking, his mouth closed. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the arrivals hall goes on, warm and soft and out of focus, its travellers small and far away, each continuing on their way. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
