A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He never faces the camera.

He is reasonable and entirely sure of himself. What he says is obvious to him, and he offers it as an explanation, not as an argument.

He answers, to someone just off screen to his RIGHT. Let his face follow the voice.

One open palm turns down toward the ground in front of him as he speaks, close to his body, and comes back.

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed, eyebrows slightly raised. He blinks, he breathes, and he expects to be agreed with.

The pavement runs on past him to the right, with parked bicycles against a plain building front and a pedestrian light further along. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the right edge of the frame, the way one looks at someone one is speaking to.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last.
