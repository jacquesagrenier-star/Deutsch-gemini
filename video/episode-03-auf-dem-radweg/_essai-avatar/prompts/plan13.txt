A static close shot holds on him, head and shoulders. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He never faces the camera.

He adds a second rule that the other man was not expecting, and he adds it as calmly as the first. He knows more than he has said.

He tells him one more thing, to someone just off screen to his LEFT. Let his face follow the voice.

Nothing else moves: his hands stay where they are and his head stays still. The stillness is the performance here.

Finally he stops speaking and holds the look a moment longer than he needs to. He blinks, he breathes, and he is waiting for the penny to drop.

Behind him the street goes on, soft and out of focus. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame, the way one looks at someone one is speaking to.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last.
