His head stays turned three quarters away from the lens, at exactly the angle it has in the image, and it holds that angle from the first frame to the last.

He is genuinely puzzled and completely friendly about it. He is not defending himself: he is trying to understand what she means.

He asks a short question, to someone just off screen to his RIGHT. Let his face follow the voice.

His head tilts a few degrees to one side as he asks, and the smile stays. His hands are out of frame and nothing else moves.

The pavement runs on past him to the right, with parked bicycles against a plain building front and a pedestrian light further along. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the right edge of the frame, the way one looks at someone one is speaking to.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last.
