A static close shot holds on him, head and shoulders. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. His head stays turned three quarters away from the lens, at exactly the angle it has in the image, and it holds that angle from the first frame to the last.

He states a plain fact. He is not making a joke, which is exactly why it is one.

He answers in one short sentence, to someone just off screen to his LEFT. Let his face follow the voice.

His face stays completely level for the whole sentence, and his hands stay exactly where they are.

Finally, and only once the sentence is finished, a small dry half-smile arrives at one corner of his mouth and stays there. He blinks, he breathes, and he lets it sit.

Behind him the street goes on, soft and out of focus. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame, the way one looks at someone one is speaking to.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last.
