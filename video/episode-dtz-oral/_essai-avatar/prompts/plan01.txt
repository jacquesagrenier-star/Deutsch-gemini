The woman in the picture sits at the table and speaks German into the lens, in a calm, warm, clear voice, as an examiner who is kind and unhurried. Let her face follow the voice. She greets and explains how the exam begins. Her hand nearer the left edge of the picture rests on the table for the whole shot. Only her other hand moves: on her last sentence it opens, palm up, a few centimetres above the table in front of her, as an invitation to begin, and it stays there, open and empty.

Across her chest we see only the plain grey-blue cardigan, open, and the plain white blouse, from the first frame to the last.

The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame she is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.

Finally, when she has finished speaking, she listens without speaking and waits for the answer: she keeps looking into the lens, she blinks, she breathes, her face stays attentive.
