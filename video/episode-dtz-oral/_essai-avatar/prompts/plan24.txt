The woman in the picture sits at the table and speaks German into the lens, in a calm, warm, clear voice, as an examiner who is kind and glad for the candidate. Let her face follow the voice, and between her sentences her warm, open smile comes back. She announces a result and encourages. Her hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays on the table. Her other hand lifts a few centimetres, and its thumb and index finger come close together, almost touching, and stay like that.

Across her chest we see only the plain grey-blue cardigan, open, and the plain white blouse, from the first frame to the last.

The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame she is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.

Finally, when she has finished speaking, she listens without speaking and waits for the answer: she keeps looking into the lens, she blinks, she breathes, she keeps her warm, open smile.
