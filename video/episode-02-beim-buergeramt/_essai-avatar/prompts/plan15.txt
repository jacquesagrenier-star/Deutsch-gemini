A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up behind the counter. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He is right of centre and never faces the camera.

He lists two documents. To him they are an ordinary pair of words.

He names what the person needs to bring, speaking to someone off screen to his
LEFT. Let his face follow the voice - the long word arrives at the end at
exactly the same pace as everything before it, with no emphasis and no pause
in front of it.

First the shorter word, then the longer one, and he does not slow down for it.
One hand may rise slightly on the counter, counting two items without actually
counting them on the fingers.

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed. He blinks and breathes. He
has no idea the second word is a problem.

He is the only person in frame. Nobody walks in from either edge, nobody passes between him and the camera, and the room behind him stays exactly as it is.
