A static close shot holds on him, head and shoulders, the counter out of frame. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He never faces the camera.

The long word has defeated him, and he knows it is a good word - he simply
cannot hold it. No shame in it: he speaks German well and this one is beyond
reach. The shot is genuinely funny and he is half in on the joke.

He starts to repeat the word, gives up in the middle, and asks again, speaking
to someone off screen to his RIGHT. Let his face follow the voice - the break
in the middle is already in the recording and it is the whole shot.

First his mouth starts the word with some confidence. Then it stops, and for a
moment nothing happens on his face at all. Only after that does the rest of
the question arrive, smaller.

Finally he stops speaking and a small helpless half-smile arrives - it must
not be there at the start and it must not be wide. He blinks, he breathes, he
waits.

He is the only person anywhere near the camera. Nobody walks into frame, nobody passes between him and the camera, and nothing in the background comes forward or grows larger.
