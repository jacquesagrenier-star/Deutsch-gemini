A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up, with the front edge of the information counter crossing the lower part of the frame. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He is left of centre and never faces the camera.

He has rehearsed this sentence on the way here and he is quietly pleased to
have it ready. Not nervous, not cocky: prepared.

He greets a stranger and states what he has come for, speaking to someone off
screen to his RIGHT, and he keeps looking that way throughout. Let his face
follow the voice - the small formality of the phrasing is already in the
recording, and his eyebrows, eyes and mouth move with the audio rather than on
their own schedule.

First a short nod of greeting, small, from the neck only. Then he says what he
wants, and one hand lifts a few centimetres from the counter and settles back
- the gesture of someone placing a request, not making a point.

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed, looking at the person off
screen. His head does not turn or wander: he blinks, he breathes, and he
expects this to be simple.

⚠ He is the ONLY person in this shot, at every single moment of it. The person he is speaking to stays completely out of frame and is NEVER seen: no head, no hair, no face, no shoulder, no arm, no back of a head, no blurred figure and no part of anyone else enters the frame at any point, at any edge, in focus or out of focus, near or far. Nobody walks in, nobody passes between him and the camera, and nothing in the background comes forward or grows larger.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last. The camera does not drift, reframe, or follow him, and the background stays where it is.
