He has asked this question many times today and it costs him nothing. Neither
friendly nor cold: occupied.

He greets the person in front of him and asks a routine question, speaking to
someone off screen to his LEFT, and he keeps looking that way. Let his face
follow the voice - the flatness is already in the recording.

First the smallest nod of greeting, barely a movement. Then the question,
while one hand stays resting near the counter and does not gesture.

Finally he stops speaking and waits for the answer, mouth closed, eyes on the
person. He blinks, he breathes, he shifts his weight once. Nothing in his face
anticipates anything: for him this is a Monday morning.

He is the only person in frame. Nobody walks in from either edge, nobody passes between him and the camera, and the room behind him stays exactly as it is.
