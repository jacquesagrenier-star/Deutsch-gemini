A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up behind the counter. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He is right of centre and never faces the camera.

He explains, briefly, and reassures. To him this really is normal, and that is
the whole point - the words stay as level as everything else he has said.

He tells the person what the document is and then says it is nothing unusual,
speaking to someone off screen to his LEFT. Let his face follow the voice.

The counter in front of him carries only the date stamp and the small
INFORMATION sign, exactly as in the image, and its wooden top stays bare and
unchanged from the first frame to the last. His hands rest on it, open and
empty, and they stay there for the whole shot; nothing is held, picked up or put down.

First the explanation, short. Then the reassurance, in exactly the same tone -
it is not kinder than the rest, it is simply the next thing he says.

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed, eyes on the person - and
for the first time in the scene his eyes stay on him half a second longer than
they need to before he looks back down at the counter. Nothing else changes:
no smile, no softening of the mouth, no sigh. It is the only moment where he
is a person rather than a desk, and it must be small enough that a viewer
could miss it. He blinks, he breathes, and he is ready for the conversation to
be over.

He is alone in the room, and the room is quiet and empty around him. To his left the counter runs on, bare except for the small INFORMATION sign and the date stamp, and behind it a plain pale-green wall with a closed noticeboard. Nothing occupies that side of the frame but the counter, the wall and the daylight. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame, the way one looks at someone one is speaking to, and they stay on it.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last. The camera does not drift, reframe, or follow him, and the background stays where it is.
