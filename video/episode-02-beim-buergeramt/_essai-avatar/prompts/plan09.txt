A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up behind the counter. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He is right of centre and never faces the camera.

He supplies a second fact, unconnected in his mind to the first. He is being
helpful, in his way.

He tells the person how long the law gives him, speaking to someone off screen
to his LEFT, and he keeps looking that way. Let his face follow the voice.

First he says it. One hand may turn briefly palm-up on the counter and come
back, a small movement that means "that is how it is written".

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed, eyes on the person. He
blinks and breathes. He does not see anything strange in what he has just
said, and nothing in his face suggests he might.

⚠ He is the ONLY person in this shot, at every single moment of it. The person he is speaking to stays completely out of frame and is NEVER seen: no head, no hair, no face, no shoulder, no arm, no back of a head, no blurred figure and no part of anyone else enters the frame at any point, at any edge, in focus or out of focus, near or far. Nobody walks in, nobody passes between him and the camera, and nothing in the background comes forward or grows larger.

The framing itself never changes: he stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same place in the frame from the first frame to the last. The camera does not drift, reframe, or follow him, and the background stays where it is.
