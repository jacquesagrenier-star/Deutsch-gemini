A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up behind the counter. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image. He is right of centre and never faces the camera.

He states a fact he states every day. He is not apologising for it and he is
not enjoying it. Completely level.

He tells the person in front of him when the first free appointment is,
speaking to someone off screen to his LEFT, and he keeps looking that way. Let
his face follow the voice - the evenness is already in the recording.

First his eyes come up from the counter to the person. Then he says it, plainly,
without slowing down on the number and without leaning on it.

Finally he stops speaking and waits, mouth closed, eyes on the person. He
blinks, he breathes, and his expression does not change at all while he waits
for the reaction. He has seen the reaction before.

He is the only person in frame. Nobody walks in from either edge, nobody passes between him and the camera, and the room behind him stays exactly as it is.
