The man in the picture sits at the table and speaks German into the lens, in a relaxed, friendly voice, as a fellow candidate planning something with a friend. Let his face follow the voice. He makes a suggestion and asks for an opinion. His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays on the table; his other hand rises a few centimetres, palm down and empty, and stays level above the wood.

Across his chest we see only the plain dark green knit of his sweater, from the first frame to the last.

The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.

Finally, when he has finished speaking, he listens without speaking and waits for the answer: he keeps looking into the lens, he blinks, he breathes, still smiling.
