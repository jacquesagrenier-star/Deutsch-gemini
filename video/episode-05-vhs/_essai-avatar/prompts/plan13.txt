A static medium shot holds on him, seen from the waist up, seated at the table. The camera is locked on a tripod and stays there for the whole shot: the same lens, the same height, the same distance. Keep his position and his distance from the camera exactly as in the image.

A real surprise: the same street. He forgets to whisper for a moment.

He asks one short question in Spanish, then answers in two short pieces, delighted. He speaks towards a point just beyond the left edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the right edge of the picture stays resting flat on the bare table, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his other hand moves: on « yo » it comes to his chest and its fingertips tap it twice, me, and then they stay resting there.

Across his chest we see only his open plain navy-blue padded jacket, the plain light-grey knit of his sweater and his fingertips resting on it, from the first frame to the last.

On the left of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the pale wooden table runs on, its surface bare, open and quiet, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and keeps laughing quietly at the coincidence, his mouth closed. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the classroom stays warm and soft and out of focus: the pale walls, the dark evening windows, the other learners still at their tables. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the left edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
