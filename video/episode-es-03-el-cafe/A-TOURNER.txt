PLAN 01   Le bar, le matin, vide -- le decor maitre
INTENTION
   A quoi sert ce plan : il ouvre l'episode (« Domingo por la mañana. Un bar
   de barrio en Madrid. ») et il FIXE le lieu pour les sept autres images, qui
   le prendront en reference.
   Ce qui doit etre lisible sans le son : un bar de quartier espagnol, le
   zinc, la machine, les tostadas, le soleil du matin. Personne dans le
   cadre : un decor vide se reference sans transporter de visage.
   Ce qui s'ajoutera : la narration, le sous-titre.
   Ce qui a deja rate : les enseignes et affiches qui ecrivent (affiche-qui-
   ecrit) -- ici PAS d'ardoise, PAS de bouteilles a etiquette, PAS de journal
   sur le zinc : on dit ce qui remplit ces places (carreaux blancs, tasses).

PROMPT D'IMAGE (Framing)
A real photograph, as a customer would take it with a good phone camera,
standing on the customer side of the counter of a small neighbourhood bar in
central Madrid on a sunny morning, looking straight at the counter. Vertical
frame.

The long bar counter is topped with dull grey zinc, worn and matte, with a
rounded front edge, and it runs across the picture from the left edge to the
right edge. On the back counter behind it, at the left end, stands a chrome
espresso machine with two spouts and bare polished sides, a row of small white
cups on white saucers warming on top of it. At the right end of the counter
sits a low glass case holding toasted half-baguettes spread with grated tomato.
Between the two, on the zinc, a square chrome napkin dispenser and a small
glass of sugar sachets in plain white paper.

Behind the counter the wall is covered in plain white tiles, and above the
tiles run two shelves of white cups and plain clear glasses. The morning sun
comes in through a street window at the right of the picture and lies across
the zinc, and the shadows of the cups and the dispenser fall towards the left.
Nobody is in the bar: it is early, quiet, ready.

The textures of real worn zinc, chrome, white glazed tile, bread crust,
tomato, glass and sunlight.
