He says « no, no » with a laugh, then exclaims two short phrases of delight. He speaks towards a point just beyond the right edge of the picture, level with his own eyes. Let his face follow the voice.

His hand nearer the left edge of the picture stays hanging at his side, exactly as in the image, for the whole shot. Only his other hand moves: on « no, no » it rises in front of his chest, palm forward, and waves twice from side to side, a few centimetres, then stays there, open, while he laughs.

Across his chest we see only his plain mid-grey knit sweater and the white collar of his T-shirt, from the first frame to the last.

On the right of the picture, between him and the edge of the frame, the zinc counter and the glass case of toast run on, bright and empty, from the first frame to the last.

Finally he stops speaking and his mouth closes into a laughing smile that stays. He blinks, he breathes, and he holds that expression the whole time he waits.

Behind him the white tiled wall of the bar and its shelves of white cups stay soft and out of focus, still, in the warm morning light. His eyes rest on a fixed point just beyond the right edge of the frame and stay there for the whole shot.

The framing holds to the very end: in the last frame he is the same size and in the same place in the picture as in the first.
