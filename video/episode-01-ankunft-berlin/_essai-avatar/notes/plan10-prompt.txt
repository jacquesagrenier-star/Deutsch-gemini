PROMPT B -- celui a coller maintenant

The man in the image, in an airport arrivals hall. Seen from the chest up, vertical framing, with the top of the information desk still in the lower part of the frame. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera movement of any kind. Keep the framing, his position and his distance from the camera exactly as they are in the image.

He is placed right of centre. The person he is speaking to stands off screen to the LEFT, and that is where he is looking. He never turns to face the camera.

She has just asked whether he is staying long, and he is telling her he starts a new job next week. He is pleased about it and a little nervous underneath, and both show.

Play it in beats. He takes a small breath before the first word. As he speaks he nods once and his chin lifts slightly - quiet confidence. Near the end of the sentence his eyes flick away for a fraction of a second, the way someone does when they hear themselves say something that matters, then come straight back to her.

The warmth reaches his EYES before it reaches his mouth. The eyes soften first; the smile follows from there and grows into something real and unforced by the end. It must not arrive already wide in the first frame and sit there unchanged.

He speaks once, in the first half of the shot, and is silent afterwards. While he is silent his mouth stays closed - but he is not still. He breathes, he blinks, he swallows once, his head settles. He is waiting for her answer, and he is interested in it.

The man in the image, in an airport arrivals hall. Seen from the chest up,
vertical framing, with the top of the information desk still in the lower

He is telling her he starts a new job next week, and he is pleased about it.
He nods once. A small lift of the chin: quiet confidence, with a trace of
nervousness under it.

His smile GROWS through the shot and ends warm and genuine - a real smile
that reaches his eyes. What it must not do is arrive already wide in the
first frame and then sit there unchanged: it builds as he speaks and opens
at the end.

He speaks once, in the first half of the shot, and is silent afterwards.
While he is silent his mouth stays closed. Through all of it the face stays
alive and engaged: the eyes, a blink, breathing, a small natural movement of
the head.

⚠️ CE PROMPT NE DIT AUCUNE SECONDE, et c'est tout l'objet de l'essai. C'est

   on interdit seulement celui qui arrive deja large a la premiere image et
   ne bouge plus.

   ⚠️ Et une mesure qui passe avant le lip-sync : une serie que personne n'a
   envie de regarder n'a pas de probleme de synchronisation.
