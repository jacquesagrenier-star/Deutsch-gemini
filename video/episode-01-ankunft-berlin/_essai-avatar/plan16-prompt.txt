PROMPT -- POUR LE 1.5 SEULEMENT.

⚠️ LE 1.0 QUICK MODE N'A PAS DE CHAMP TEXTE. Sa fiche ne liste que deux
   entrees : image + audio. Le prompt est precisement ce que la 1.5 a
   ajoute. Sur le 1.0, il n'y a rien a coller -- deux champs, et c'est
   tout.

The man in the image, in an airport arrivals hall. Head and shoulders,
vertical framing. Locked-off camera: no zoom, no push-in, no camera
movement of any kind. Keep the framing, his position and his distance
from the camera exactly as they are in the image.

He looks toward the LEFT of the frame, at the person he is speaking to,
off screen. He never turns to face the camera.

He is thanking her, warm and genuine. A small nod of thanks. His
shoulders loosen: he has just been told where to go. He has come off a
night flight and is a little tired under the politeness.

The smile arrives only at the END, and it is small and closed-lipped.
Do not smile through the shot. Do not show a broad grin and do not show
rows of teeth at any point.

He speaks only once, briefly, near the beginning. He is silent for most
of the shot, and while he is silent his mouth stays closed. Between and
after the words the face stays alive: the eyes, a blink, breathing, a
small natural movement of the head.
